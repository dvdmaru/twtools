Job hunting guide for fresh graduates in 2026: If your first resume is written like this, the chance of HR taking the initiative to schedule an interview is 3 times higher

workplace skills · May 17, 2026 · 10 min

The graduation season is about to begin in June 2026, and it is estimated that more than 220,000 fresh graduates will enter the job market in Taiwan. If you haven’t prepared a resume or submitted 30 submissions but no one responded, the problem is often not that you are not good enough, but that the resume did not grab HR’s attention in the first 3 seconds. From resume structure, autobiography writing to customization techniques, this article gives you a job search blueprint for “asking for an interview proactively.” The information in this article is updated as of May 2026.

The 3-second rule for resumes: Will HR really read it carefully?

According to the Human Bank survey, the average time it takes HR to review a resume is 6 to 10 seconds, and 70-80% of resumes will be deleted in the first round. In other words, your resume only has 6-10 seconds to fight for the opportunity to “enter the second round.”

Grasp 3 principles:

  • Focus on the first 1/3 of the page: title, job application, academic qualifications, and 1-2 most representative experiences, within the range that can be seen without scrolling.
  • Numbers are greater than adjectives: “Assisting in marketing activities” is very vague, “Assisting in organizing offline lectures for 200 people, and posts reaching over 50,000” is specific.
  • White space and font: Use 11-12pt font level, 1.5 line spacing, and 2cm left and right white space. Only when it looks breathable will you want to read it.

If you haven’t made a formal resume yet, you can first use the resume generator to fill in the basic information and quickly produce a standard version. This saves you time on typesetting. Write the content first, and then slowly adjust the visuals.

5 alternative experiences that you can write about without internship experience

The most common anxiety among fresh graduates is “I don’t have an internship”, but HR actually looks at “what you have done that shows your ability”, and it does not necessarily have to be an internship. You can write about the following 5 experiences:

  • Class Topic: Quantify results. For example, “Market Research Project: Interview 30 consumers, produce a 40-page report, and get the highest score in the class.”
  • Society Cadres: Emphasis on leadership and cross-group communication. For example, “Served as the activity director of the department society, hosted 300 people to welcome new students, controlled the budget within 80,000, and achieved a satisfaction level of 4.6/5.”
  • Work experience: You can also write in the service industry. For example, “2 years of experience in a beverage store, assisted in training 5 new employees, and increased monthly sales by 12%.”
  • Self-study courses: Certificates from Coursera, Hahow, and Udemy count. The key point is “what you learned and which project you used it in”, not just posting the certificate link.
  • Personal works: GitHub, Behance, IG, blogs, and podcasts can all be listed. Creative work especially requires personal works.

The key point is to “use specific numbers to illustrate the impact.” If you really don’t have numbers to write down, write “execution details”: when, how many times it was done, how many people it was done with.

How to write an autobiography that doesn’t look like a running account: STAR Rule in practice

The STAR rule is the most practical structure when writing about experiences:

  • S (Situation): the background at the time and the problems faced
  • T (Task): The specific task you are assigned or take the initiative to undertake
  • A (Action): What did you do and how did you do it?
  • R (Result): What measurable impact is produced

Example (bad way of writing): “I serve as an activity leader in the department society, organized many activities, and learned a lot of communication skills.”

Example (rewritten by STAR): “As a junior, I served as the long-term organizer of department society activities (S) and was responsible for hosting the orientation of 300 people (T). I prepared 2 months in advance, recruited a team of 15 people to divide the work, tracked the progress with Trello, and negotiated a 20% discount with 3 vendors (A). The final budget was controlled at 85% of the original, and the student satisfaction rate was 4.6/5, which is the highest in the past 3 years (R).”

The recommended word count for your autobiography is 500-800 words, divided into 3 paragraphs: the first paragraph directly writes the position you are applying for and the strongest proof of ability; the second paragraph writes 1-2 representative experiences using STAR; the third paragraph writes your specific understanding and expectations of this position and this company. Avoid starting with childhood memories like “I come from a warm family in Taichung.” HR has no time to read.

What should be customized when applying for different positions?

Applying to 50 companies with the same resume is a big no-no. Spend 5-10 minutes doing these 3 things before submitting your resume each time:

  • Find out the keywords of the job vacancy: For example, if you are looking for “digital marketing specialist”, “FB advertising”, “GA”, “copywriting” and “KPI” often appear in JD. These words should naturally appear in your resume and autobiography, because many companies will use ATS systems to do keyword screening first.
  • Modify resume title and first paragraph of autobiography: Change the title from “Personal Resume” to “Application for Digital Marketing Specialist of OO Company - Wang Xiaoming”, and directly write “Why am I suitable for this position” in the first paragraph of the autobiography.
  • Reorder experience: Put experience related to the position you are applying for first, and shorten or delete irrelevant experience. For example, if you are applying for an engineer job, put your community experience in one line and put the topic and GitHub links at the top.

Use a Chinese typo checker to scan your resume and autobiography before sending it out. Typos are the most likely points to be deducted, especially typos in the job title and company name you are applying for, which is almost equal to being eliminated immediately.

4 common reasons for no response after submitting resume

I haven’t received any response after a month of submitting. Please check these 4 points first:

  • The match between academic experience and position is less than 60%: For example, if you apply for a “senior marketing specialist” that requires 1 year of experience, fresh graduates will be automatically rejected. Start with the positions open to fresh graduates such as “junior”, “reserve cadre” and “assistant”.
  • Resume format running, the file is too large: Word will run on different computers, save it as PDF and then submit. The file size should be controlled within 2MB. Files that are too large may be blocked by the mailbox.
  • Autobiography writes about family background rather than ability: “My parents taught me to be responsible since I was a child.” HR really doesn’t want to read this kind of sentence.
  • Contact hours are not suitable: If you cannot answer the phone number on your resume during class during the day, write down a time period that is convenient for contact, such as “You can answer the phone after 17:00 on weekdays.”

Timing for active follow-up: If there is no response within 7-10 working days after submission, you can write a short inquiry letter, reiterate your interest and introduction, attach your resume and send it again. Don’t make the letter too long, 3-5 sentences is enough.

FAQ

Do I need to include a photo on my resume?

Depends on the industry. Most traditional industries, service industries, and financial industries require photos, while those in the technology industry and foreign investors are optional. If you do, be sure to use ID photos taken within the past year (white background, front face, natural expression). Do not use travel photos or social media photos.

Do you need to write if your GPA is not good?

It’s okay not to write if it’s lower than 3.0, most companies won’t take the initiative to ask. However, if you are from a prestigious university or related department, and you are applying for a position that requires an academic background (such as research, consultant), it is recommended to write it down and strengthen other highlights.

Do you want to make a self-introduction video?

If the job vacancy does not require it, you will not get too many points if you do it, but it will take time. Unless you are applying for positions in marketing, new media, PR, etc. that require a statement, a 1-2 minute self-introduction video will be a bonus.

Which is the best day for delivery?

According to Labor Bank statistics, the highest proportion of HR personnel looking at resumes is between 9 and 11 a.m. on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Mondays are usually spent dealing with matters accumulated over the weekend, and Fridays are spent preparing to take the holidays, which is less efficient.

5-minute checklist before submitting your resume: Is the title the correct position name, is the photo within 1 year, scan it for typos, the PDF format should be less than 2MB, and the email should not be a middle school nickname (it is recommended to use the format of “English name. Last name@gmail.com”). Starting today, revise your resume to the point where HR can take the initiative to make an appointment with you, and your job search will be much smoother.
