2026 Taiwan Summer Electricity Rates Start June 1: Correct Bracket Tiers + NT$12,000 Subsidy + 7 Saving Tips

Life Tips · May 3, 2026 · 9 min

**The information in this article was updated as of May 2026. ** Taipower’s “Summer Month Electricity Price” is implemented from June 1 to September 30 every year, which is much more expensive than during non-summer months. For working families, just by turning on the air conditioner, the electricity bill in June may be 1.5 times higher than in May. This article compiles the latest electricity price range in 2026, practical power-saving tips, and energy-saving subsidies of up to 12,000 yuan to help you prepare before the summer months come.

Key points of the progressive system: It is not “the highest unit price for all electricity consumption”, but “tiered billing”. For example, if you use 500 degrees, the first 120 degrees will cost 1.68 yuan, 121-330 degrees will cost 2.45 yuan, and 331-500 degrees will cost 3.70 yuan.

Assume your monthly electricity consumption is 500 kWh:

Electricity bill for non-summer months:

  • 1-120 degrees: 120 × 1.68 = 201.6 yuan
  • 121-330 degrees: 210 × 2.16 = 453.6 yuan
  • 331-500 degrees: 170 × 3.03 = 515.1 yuan
  • Total about 1,170 yuan

  • 1-120 degrees: 120 × 1.68 = 201.6 yuan
  • 121-330 degrees: 210 × 2.45 = 514.5 yuan
  • 331-500 degrees: 170 × 3.70 = 629 yuan
  • Total approximately 1,345 yuan

Summer months cost $175 more than non-summer months (+15%). If the electricity consumption reaches 700 degrees, the summer month will cost 400-500 yuan more than the non-summer month; the difference will be even greater when the electricity is above 1000 degrees. Save more than 331 kilowatt hours of electricity, the highest benefit.

Tip 1: Air conditioner setting 26-28 degrees + electric fan assist

For every 1 degree increase in air conditioning, you can save approximately 6% on electricity bills (data from the Energy Administration of the Ministry of Economic Affairs). The best setting is 26-28 degrees for the electric fan. The body temperature is almost the same as setting it to 24 degrees, but the monthly electricity bill can be saved by 200-400 yuan.

Key points for saving energy in air conditioners:

  • Clean the filter regularly (dirty filter consumes 10-15% more power)
  • Close doors and windows, and draw curtains to block sunlight (Walls exposed to the west should be protected even more)
  • Use “Auto” mode to save power than “Strong Cooling”
  • Don’t switch the variable frequency air conditioner on and off frequently, it will save more money if it runs continuously for 4-6 hours.
  • Air conditioner installation location avoid west-facing walls and the outdoor unit should be well ventilated
  • Electric Fan Assist: Creates air convection, reducing body temperature by 2-3 degrees

Tip 2: Find out the hidden power consumption of standby appliances

Standby power (commonly known as vampire power) accounts for about 5-10% of household electricity consumption. The most common hidden power consumers:

-Set-top box, Wi-Fi router (24 hours on)

  • Chargers and transformers that are still plugged in when not at home
  • Microwave and oven with clock display
  • Standby lights for TVs, stereos, and computer screens
  • Water dispenser, smart speaker
  • Printers and scanners (the standby power consumption is higher than expected)

Implementation Suggestion: Buy an “extension cord with switch” (200-500 yuan per set), and cut off unnecessary electrical appliances at once after get off work or when going out. According to actual measurements, an ordinary family can save 100-200 yuan on electricity bills a month, which is more than enough to pay back the cost in one year.

Step 3: Apply for “Residential Time Electricity Price” Assessment

If your household’s electricity consumption pattern is “no one goes to work during the day and only uses it when going home at night”, you can apply for Taipower’s “Residential Time Electricity Price.” Off-peak hours (10:30 pm to 7:30 the next day) The electricity price drops to as low as about 1.96 yuan per kilowatt hour. Washing, drying, cooking, and charging electric vehicles are all arranged during this time, and monthly savings start from 300 yuan.

⚠️ Trial Calculation is Important: First use the Taipower App’s “Electricity Bill Trial Calculation” tool to evaluate the electricity consumption pattern in the past 6 months to avoid expensive replacement. Some large electricity consumers in Shuangbei are suitable to maintain the original plan after trial calculations.

No. 4: Seize the 2026 energy-saving subsidy + goods tax refund (up to 12,000 yuan)

In 2026, the Ministry of Economic Affairs’ “replacement of old residential appliances with new ones” + the Ministry of Finance’s “excise tax reduction” dual subsidies will be launched simultaneously. The following is the combined amount:

Up to NT$5,000 for a single air conditioner

  • Energy-saving subsidy: 3,000 yuan per level 1 energy-efficient air conditioner (Ministry of Economic Affairs save3000.moeaea.gov.tw)
  • Goods tax refund: further refund 2,000 yuan (Ministry of Finance)
  • Total maximum of NT$5,000

Up to RMB 12,000 for a complete set of home appliances

Buy 4 major appliances including air conditioners, refrigerators, water heaters, and dehumidifiers, and apply for subsidies + tax rebates for each, total up to 12,000 yuan:

After replacement, the electricity bill will be paid back in ** one year**. Each county and city has additional subsidies (for example, Taipei City provides an additional NT$1,000-2,000), which can be found on the official website of the “Ministry of Economic Affairs’ Energy-Saving Home Appliances Subsidy.”

Tip 5: Use accounting to track monthly electricity bills

Without accounting, there is no way to review results. suggestion:

  1. Monthly meter reading (based on bimonthly bills), write down the kWh, and use a spreadsheet to track the “monthly electricity consumption” and “unit price per kWh”
  2. Taipower App Smart Meter allows you to view the hourly power consumption curve and find out which hours are the peak power consumption
  3. Compare to the same month last year: See how much progress you have made compared to the same month last year

If you need to quickly calculate electricity bill conversion or units, you can use the Unit Conversion Tool to handle the conversion of degrees, watts, and kilowatt hours. If you want to know how many days until today the next meter reading is, you can use the Date Calculator and the Countdown Timer to remind yourself to check the meter every two months.

Tip 6: Change LED bulbs + smart sockets

Many homes still use traditional light bulbs or T8 fluorescent tubes. Change to LED:

  • An 8W LED replaces a 60W tungsten bulb, reducing power consumption by 86%
  • An 18W LED tube replaces a 40W T8 fluorescent tube, reducing power consumption by 55%
  • The average lifespan is 5-10 times longer, and the frequency of lamp replacement is reduced
  • One LED costs 100-300 yuan, and the money will be paid back within half a year.

Smart Socket (200-500 yuan each) allows you to use a mobile app to control the switch of electrical appliances. If you forget to turn off the air conditioner when you go out, you can turn it off remotely.

Tip 7: Do laundry and dishwashing during concentrated periods

Many people don’t know that washing machines + dryers are the invisible killers of summer electricity bills:

  • Laundry Concentration: Wash twice a week, fully loaded each time, saving 50% of electricity compared to washing 1 piece of clothing every day
  • Cold water washing: 90% of the clothes are washed in cold water. The heating power consumption of hot water washing accounts for 80% of the total power consumption of the washing machine.
  • Hanging clothes instead of drying them: In summer, it can be dried in 2-3 hours on a sunny day, which can save about 5-10 kilowatt-hours of electricity each time in the dryer.

Smart meter + time electricity price: full explanation of applicable conditions in 2026

Time-based electricity prices (using different unit prices during the day/night) can save electricity bills, but the prerequisite is that your home must first have a “smart meter”. Taipower’s construction progress is: A total of 6 million households will be completed in 2030 (119), and 100% will be fully deployed in 2035 (124). As of the end of 2025, 76,000 residential households and 137,000 small shops in Taiwan have adopted time-based electricity prices, totaling 213,000 households.

Currently, you cannot actively apply for smart meters. Taipower will actively install them in only two situations: ① Apply for the “time-based electricity price plan” ② Your home has “renewable energy connected to the grid” (such as your own rooftop solar energy). General users have to wait for the comprehensive replacement schedule from 2030 to 2035.

Is time-based electricity price suitable for you? Simple assessment:

  • Household electricity consumption More than 700 kilowatt hours per month + High proportion of electricity consumption at night → Applicable, potential annual savings of 1,000-3,000 yuan
  • Business electricity consumption More than 1,100 kWh per month → Applicable
  • Low power consumption / peak usage during the day → Not recommended, it may be more expensive

Application channels: ① Over the counter at Taipower Service Center ② Apply online on Taipower website. It is recommended to use the Taipower App to try out the last 6 months of bills before making a decision because it can only be changed once in a year.

4 things you can do before the end of May

Summer electricity prices start on 6/1, it’s not too late to start preparing now:

  1. Air conditioner inventory: Arrange the replacement schedule for the air conditioners in your home that are over 8 years old, and apply for subsidies accordingly
  2. Master switch extension cord: Connect all unnecessary standby appliances to the switch extension cord
  3. Time electricity price trial calculation: Download Taipower App and log in to check the time electricity price trial calculation result.
  4. Energy-saving subsidy delivery: Purchase + delivery 6 months ago to avoid running out of quota (2026 subsidy quota is limited)

After doing these four things, you can save 1,000-3,000 yuan from June to September alone.

FAQ

Q1: When will the summer electricity price end? A: It ends on September 30th every year, and the non-summer electricity prices will resume on October 1st. However, the bill will be calculated across months, and the bill received at the end of October may still include the summer electricity price.

**Q2: Is it more economical to turn the air conditioner on all day or switch it in and out? ** A: If variable frequency air conditioner operates continuously for 4-6 hours, it is more economical to turn it on “continuously on”; fixed frequency air conditioner is not recommended to be turned off when going out for a short time (within 30 minutes). You should turn it off when you are out for a long time (more than 2 hours), otherwise it will be a waste of electricity.

Q3: Does it cost money to apply for electricity price during the time? A: It is free to apply, but it can only be changed once within 1 year. It is also free to change back to the original plan, but you need to wait for the cooling-off period. It is recommended to use the Taipower App to test for 6 months before making a decision.

Q4: Will the price increase for large electricity consumers? A: For residential electricity consumption exceeding 700 kilowatt hours in a single month, the price is 5.04 yuan per kilowatt hour in summer months and 4.14 yuan per kilowatt hour in non-summer months. If it exceeds 1000 degrees, it will be more expensive (from 6.24 yuan in Xia Yue). Controlling the temperature within 700 degrees can avoid the highest level. Households with multiple people may consider applying for electric meters per household.

Q5: How to apply for the energy saving subsidy of 3,000 yuan? A: ① Purchase level 1 energy-efficiency appliances and keep the invoices ② Complete the recycling of old appliances (obtain the waste recycling slip) ③ Submit online to save3000.moeaea.gov.tw + upload ID card, passbook, and electricity bill receipt ④ Wait for the approved subsidy to be credited to your account (about 2-4 weeks).
