2026 Comprehensive Tax Refund Inquiry Guide: When will it be credited? What should I do if I don’t receive it?

tax knowledge · May 10, 2026 · 9 min

Every year after May, what office workers miss most is the day when the comprehensive income tax refund is credited to their accounts. The comprehensive income tax filing period for 2026 is from May 1 to May 31. According to the annual operating rhythm of the National Taxation Bureau, the first batch of tax refund remittances will begin as soon as July 31. But you know what? The tax refund method is different, the filing time is different, and the account date may differ by two or three months.

This article organizes the 2026 tax refund enquiry, entry schedule, and replacement process, and shares how to use the tax refund after it is pocketed, so that the money will not only be credited for a while, but actually help you save it. Information updated as of May 2026.

2026 tax filing season tax refund schedule for lazy people

The annual tax refund work of the Ministry of Finance and the State Administration of Taxation is divided into three levels according to the filing time and filing method. This year’s schedule is as follows:

  • First batch of tax refund: July 31, 2026 - Those who completed online declaration from May 1 to May 10 and met the conditions for automatic verification
  • Second batch of tax refund: October 31, 2026 - Those who completed the declaration from May 11 to May 31, or those who did not remit the first batch
  • The third batch of tax refunds: January 20, 2027 - Manual review or supplementary cases

The earlier you apply, the better your chance of being among the first batch. If you have missed the early bird window on May 10, don’t worry too much. As long as you complete it before May 31, you will still receive the second batch.

There is a big difference between the three tax refund methods.

You will choose the tax refund method when filing. The time and convenience of different methods vary greatly:

1. Direct tax refund (recommended)

Get your tax refund directly into your designated bank account without having to go to the bank or cash a check. About 95% of taxpayers choose this method every year, which is the fastest and most convenient. If the account number you filled in this year is wrong, or the account has been deactivated, the IRS will use other methods to process it and postpone it to the next batch.

2. Voucher redemption

The IRS will send a tax refund voucher to your registered address, and you need to bring your ID card to a designated bank to redeem it. If you are often away from your place of residence and the voucher is lost, you have to apply for a reissue again. This step alone can take a month.

3. Offset income tax for the following year

Saving this year’s tax refund against next year’s tax payable is suitable for people who are sure they will pay taxes next year. In practice, this option is rarely chosen because the money stuck with the IRS has no interest.

How to check the progress of tax refund? Ministry of Finance Query System Tutorial

Starting from July 2026, you can check the progress of your tax refund through the following three channels:

  • Ministry of Finance Tax Portal (tax.nat.gov.tw): After logging in, click “Comprehensive Income Tax Refund Inquiry” and enter your ID number, date of birth, and filing year.
  • Official websites of various district taxation agencies: For example, the Taipei State Taxation Bureau and the North District State Taxation Bureau have independent tax refund inquiry pages.
  • Telephone inquiry: Dial the toll-free hotline 0800-000-321 and follow the voice instructions

Three statuses will be displayed when querying: “Under Approval” means it is still under review; “Appropriated” means the bank has received the instruction, and it will usually be credited within 1-3 working days; “Rejected” means further processing is required.

Common reasons and solutions for not receiving tax refunds

Every year, many people have not received their tax refund in August. There are four main reasons:

  • The bank account number is filled in incorrectly: one digit is missing, one digit is more, or a closed account number is filled in. The solution is to correct the account number on the tax portal, or call the IRS for assistance.
  • Return of voucher with incorrect address: Choose voucher redemption but the household registration address has changed. The solution is to bring your ID card to the household registration office to change your address, and then ask the IRS to reissue it.
  • There are accumulated taxes and fees that have been offset: The arrears of housing tax, land price tax, and health insurance premiums will be directly deducted from the tax refund. The solution is to check the tax arrears details on the tax portal.
  • The declaration materials are manually reviewed if there are any doubts: For example, the amount of deductions listed is abnormal. The solution is to wait patiently for the third batch of tax refunds, or take the initiative to make replacements to speed up the process.

Smart money use strategies after tax refund is credited

Many people spend their tax refunds as soon as they come in—for a celebration dinner, a new cell phone, or a new appliance. This amount of money averages 15,000 to 30,000 yuan per person. If it is paid in one lump sum, it is equivalent to paying in vain for a year. It is recommended to divide it into three parts:

  • 50% into emergency reserve: put it in a digital account with high interest rates, such as Taishin Richart and Wing Fung DAWHO, with an annual interest rate of about 1.5-2%
  • 30% Invest in yourself: Sign up for language courses, take certificate exams, and buy books. This is the most cost-effective investment.
  • 20% Enjoy Life: Eat a good meal, buy things you have always wanted to buy, and reward yourself for a year of hard work

If you plan to use your tax refund to plan overseas travel or large-scale expenditures, you can make good use of online tools to calculate the real-time exchange rate to find the best time to buy foreign exchange, and then use the date calculator to plan the number of departure and return days to avoid impulsive spending.

Will tax be pursued if income is underreported? Supplementary declaration practice

If you find that dividends, rent, and part-time income were underreported last year, don’t take any chances. The National Taxation Bureau has a “cash flow verification system” that compares bank incoming and outgoing accounts and securities dealer transaction details. Once an underreporting is found, in addition to back payment of taxes, a fine of up to 2 times plus late payment fees will also be levied.

The correct approach is to take the initiative to go to the IRS to apply for an “automatic supplementary report”. If you turn yourself in before being caught, you will be exempted from penalties in accordance with Article 48-1 of the Tax Collection Act, as long as you pay the back tax and accrued interest (the annual interest rate is approximately 1.5%). For a tax evasion of 50,000 yuan, if you take the initiative to make a supplementary report, you may only pay a few hundred yuan more in interest. Compared with being caught and fined tens of thousands of yuan, there is a big difference.

FAQ

Q1: When will the 2026 tax refund be credited to my account at the earliest?

The first batch is July 31, provided you complete your online declaration before May 10 and choose a direct tax refund. If you apply at the end of May, you have to wait until October 31 for the second batch.

Q2: Will the tax refund amount be deducted by the bank?

Won’t. The direct transfer of tax refunds from the IRS is official remittance, and all banks do not charge handling fees. You can remit whatever amount you want.

Q3: If the household registration is moved but the address is not changed, will the tax refund voucher be lost?

meeting. The National Taxation Bureau is based on household registration information. It is recommended to confirm the household registration address before filing, or directly choose the direct tax refund to avoid mailing problems.

Q4: If I forget to declare, can I still make it up?

You can still file after May 31, but you will be charged a late fee (1% for every 2 days exceeded, up to a maximum of 15%) and interest. If it is time for a tax refund, you will still get the money if you make a supplementary declaration, but the time will be extended to the first batch the following year.
