14 days until 2026 tax filing: A complete guide to extension applications, tax saving tips and tax refund inquiries before the 5/31 deadline

tax knowledge · May 17, 2026 · 10 min

The 2026 comprehensive income tax filing period ends on May 31, with only 14 days left. If you haven’t filed your taxes yet, want to make corrections after filing, or want to know when your tax refund will be credited, this article will make it clear once and for all. From online filing, extension applications, last-minute tax-saving tips, to how to check your tax refund in July, follow these steps without panic. The information in this article is updated as of May 2026.

The deadline is 14 days away: What should I do if I haven’t paid my taxes or can’t file them?

The comprehensive income tax filing period for 2026 is from May 1st to May 31st. Failure to file by the due date will impose a late payment fee of 1% of the tax payable every day, up to a maximum of 15%. If it is not paid for more than 30 days, the IRS will directly determine the tax amount and pursue the payment. At that time, there is no room for defense.

If you haven’t done it yet, there are two ways now: the first is to declare directly online, and the second is to apply for an extension. Online declaration does not require a card reader and can be completed with a health insurance card + registration password, mobile phone authentication, or the Ministry of Finance’s Tax Action Service App. It is recommended to use the “Comprehensive Income Tax Electronic Settlement Declaration and Payment System” of the Ministry of Finance. After logging in, the system will automatically bring in withholding vouchers, insurance premiums, donations, medical and other information. Most people can complete it in 15 minutes.

For those who do not have a tax payment password, you can use “health insurance card + registration password” as the fastest way to complete the registration on the spot using your mobile phone. If you prefer the physical process, KIOSK machines in nearby supermarkets can also print the resulting list and take it home for comparison. You can use a date calculator to calculate how many days are left until 5/31, and set your alarm before 5/29 to avoid heavy traffic jams on the system on the last day.

It’s really too late: 3 steps to apply for tax deferral online

If after trial calculation, you find that the amount to be paid back is very large and cannot be paid at once, you can apply for payment in installments, up to 36 installments, with the amount of each installment not less than 3,000 yuan. The interest rate is calculated based on the one-year postal savings deposit interest rate, which is approximately 1.6% in 2026, which is much lower than the recurring credit card interest rate.

Three steps to apply for installment:

  • Check installments when filing online: When selecting the tax payment method in the electronic declaration system, directly check “Apply for installment tax payment”, and the system will calculate the amount of each installment.
  • Select the number of installments: The tax payable can be divided into 2-3 installments if the tax payable is less than 20,000, 6 installments can be divided if the tax is less than 50,000, and the maximum can be 36 installments. The more installments, the more interest per installment.
  • Payment after approval: The IRS usually approves it within 2-4 weeks. After approval, payment notes for each installment will be sent. Remember to pay on time using an ATM or credit card. If one installment is overdue, you will lose your installment qualification.

Note that installments are applicable to “back tax payment” and tax refund households do not need to apply. If there happens to be a major change in your family (such as job loss, serious illness), you can also apply for an “extension” for up to 1 year. You must apply to the IRS in your area with supporting documents.

Tax-saving tips that can still be used in the last week

Many people forget that standard deductions and itemized deductions can be “cherished.” In 2026, the standard deduction for singles is 124,000 and for couples combined, 248,000. If you have the following documents that add up to more than this amount, it is definitely a good deal to enumerate:

  • Medical Expenses: Medical receipts for yourself, your spouse, and dependent relatives (including orthodontics, long-term care, maternity, and health examinations). There is no upper limit for verification.
  • Insurance premium: personal insurance, labor health insurance, and national pension, with an annual upper limit of 24,000 yuan per person.
  • Donations: Donations to education, culture, public welfare, and charity, with a maximum of 20% of total comprehensive income; there is no upper limit for donations to the government.
  • Interest on borrowing money for home purchase for self-use: up to 300,000 per household.
  • House rent: If you or your spouse rent a house to live in the Republic of China, the upper limit is 120,000 per household.

Also a reminder, the Special Preschool Deduction will be relaxed to “150,000 per child under 8 years old” and 225,000 per person for the second and above starting from 2024. Many novice parents will miss this.

How to check the tax refund inquiry: 2026 tax refund 3-stage schedule

The tax refund for 2026 will be credited in three batches:

  • Batch 1: July 31 (those who completed the declaration before 5/10 and chose direct tax refund)
  • Batch 2: October 31 (those who apply after May 10, or apply in writing)
  • Batch 3: January 20 of the following year (special cases, including supplementary documents and replies)

Inquiry method: Go to the “Tax Portal of the Ministry of Finance” and enter your ID number and date of birth to check. As for the tax refund method, it is recommended to choose “direct tax refund” and deposit it into your bank account, which is more trouble-free than paper checks. If you have not received your tax refund by early August, first check whether your household address has been changed and whether your account number has been entered incorrectly, and then call the IRS service hotline 0800-000-321 to inquire.

Please note: The IRS will not call you to go to an ATM to get a tax refund. Any call asking for money transfer or account and password is a scam. If you encounter one, please hang up and dial 165.

How to remedy the wrong declaration method: Tutorial on correction of comprehensive income tax declaration

What should I do if I find after filing that I have underreported my income, omitted deductions, or selected the wrong applicable tax rate? There are two situations:

Discovered before 5/31: Just re-enter the electronic declaration system and overwrite the original declaration information. The system will take the last one as the criterion. If the declaration is made in writing, you need to resubmit it to the local IRS.

Discovered after 5/31: We need to go through the “Correction Report” procedure. If you have “overpaid”, you can fill out a tax refund application form and attach the calculation formula, and you can apply for a tax refund within 5 years; if you have “underpaid”, the sooner you voluntarily make up the payment, the better. Active overpayment usually only accrues interest, and you will not be punished until you are found out.

Common correction situations include: forgetting to list dependent relatives, omitting to report dividend income, insurance premiums exceeding the upper limit, forgetting to list medical receipts, etc. It is recommended that all documents be retained for at least 5 years after filing. The IRS has a 5-year review period.

FAQ

Will I have to pay a lot of taxes with a monthly salary of 40,000?

For a single office worker with an annual salary of RMB 480,000, after deducting the tax exemption of RMB 97,000, the standard deduction of RMB 124,000, and the salary deduction of RMB 218,000, the taxable income is approximately RMB 41,000. A 5% tax rate applies, and the tax amount is approximately RMB 2,000. In most cases, if the withholding amount has been exceeded, a tax refund of several thousand to tens of thousands of yuan can be obtained.

Is it cost-effective to pay taxes by credit card?

Look at bank offers. Most credit cards offer 1.5%-3% rebate for tax payments, which is usually still earned after deducting handling fees. However, it should be noted that most credit card tax payments do not count towards the “general consumption” threshold. There are no mileage or bonus points added, and they are purely reward money.

Will I be notified when the tax refund is credited?

meeting. After the direct tax refund is credited, a notification letter will be sent to the household address, and the bank will also send an app to promote it. However, fraud groups have recently sent text messages pretending to be the IRS asking you to click on a link. Remember to only look at the official inquiry page of the Ministry of Finance’s tax portal.

How to report those who leave in the middle of the year?

When you leave your job, the company will issue a “Various Income Withholding Voucher” listing all your salary from this year to the date of resignation. If you change jobs in the middle of the year, the new company will also open one, and the system will automatically compile it when reporting. Remember to check the amount against the original salary slip.

Do it now: Go to the Ministry of Finance’s electronic declaration system to do a trial calculation for 15 minutes to see if it’s a tax payment or a tax refund. If you need to pay back taxes and the amount is large, apply for installment today; if you can get a tax refund, remember to choose direct transfer to your regular account. There are 14 days left. The earlier you file, the sooner you will receive your tax refund, and the sooner you will be free from the tax filing process.
