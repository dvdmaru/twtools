2026 Taiwan Income Tax Prep Guide: 8 Steps for Office Workers to Save Time and Tax

Tax & Finance · April 12, 2026 · 11 min

The tax filing season is counting down to May, are you ready?

May is the month for filing comprehensive income tax every year, and April is the best time to prepare. According to statistics from the Ministry of Finance, more than 6 million households need to file a comprehensive income tax declaration every year, but many people always postpone it until the last minute. As a result, they either omit their income or forget to deduct items and pay more tax in vain. This article helps you get everything you need to prepare before tax filing season starts. The information in this article is updated as of May 2026.

Step 1: Confirm your reporting identity and login method

The first step in filing a tax return is to find out your filing status. If you are single and an office worker without dependent relatives, you usually file a “single individual declaration”. If you are married, the couple must file a joint return, but you can choose “joint tax calculation” or “separate tax calculation”. Which one is more cost-effective depends on the income gap between the two parties (the tax filing software will automatically try to calculate the three methods).

The Ministry of Finance’s “Comprehensive Electronic Settlement of Income Tax Declaration and Tax Payment System” has 6 login methods: ① Mobile phone authentication (requires personal account number) ② Health insurance card + registration password (requires card reader) ③ Natural person certificate (the most complete download information) ④ Mobile natural person certificate (fingerprint/face recognition) ⑤ Account number + inquiry code (free printing at supermarkets) ⑥ Financial certificate.

For most office workers, mobile natural person certificate is the most convenient (set once and use it every year, no tools are required), followed by mobile phone authentication (fastest 30 seconds). It is recommended to confirm in advance whether your natural person certificate has expired (valid for 5 years) and whether the health insurance card has been registered with the password on the National Health Insurance Agency website. ⚠️ QR Code is a barcode for payment (scan to pay tax with a credit card or debit card), not a login method, don’t be confused.

Step 2: Download or query the obtained information

Starting from April 28, you can use the Ministry of Finance’s electronic tax declaration and payment system to check last year’s income information using your natural person certificate or health insurance card. The system will list your salary income, interest income, dividend income, rental income, etc. But be aware that some income may not appear in the system, for example:

  • Overseas income (overseas investment, overseas projects, overseas rental income)
  • Proceeds from private transactions (property transactions other than selling cars, houses, and stocks)
  • Part of part-time job or case-taking income (the other party did not declare withholding)
  • Side business income (Shopee stores, podcast advertising profit sharing, industry distribution), but starting from 2026, many platforms have begun to collect data

It is recommended that you dig out last year’s salary slips and bank statements and compare them one by one to see if the system information is complete. If you usually use Exchange Rate Conversion Tool to handle foreign currency income, don’t forget to record the converted amount in Taiwan dollars.

Step 3: Take stock of your deductions and save what you can

Deductions are key to how much tax you pay. The standard deduction for 2025 (filing in 2026) is 131,000 yuan for singles and 262,000 yuan for couples. But if your itemized deductions add up to more than the standard deduction, you should itemize. Common itemized deductions include:

  • Insurance premium: up to 24,000 yuan per person per year (life insurance). There is no limit on the amount of national health insurance premiums, and the full amount is deducted
  • Medical and maternity expenses: registration fees, hospitalization fees, surgery fees, etc., the portion exceeding the insurance benefits can be deducted (limited to health insurance contracted hospitals)
  • Interest on borrowing money for home purchase: up to 300,000 yuan per year
  • Donations: Donations to the government or educational and cultural charity organizations, up to 20% of total comprehensive income

⚠️ Housing rent has been changed from enumeration to “special deduction” (new system starting from 2013), with a maximum limit of 180,000 yuan per household. Renters must pay attention to switching to the special deduction route (explained in the next step).

Now sort out last year’s insurance premium receipts, medical expense receipts, and rent remittance records, and calculate whether the itemized deductions exceed the standard deduction.

Step 4: Don’t forget the special deduction (2026 figures have been updated)

In addition to the general deductions, there are several “special deductions” that many people ignore, and there will be major adjustments in 2026 (year 114):

  • Special deduction for salary income: 218,000 yuan per person
  • Special deduction for savings investment: bank interest income is tax-free up to 270,000 yuan per household
  • Special deduction for people with disabilities: NT$218,000 per person
  • Special deduction for education tuition: 25,000 yuan per child for children studying at junior college or above
  • Special deduction for preschool children (✅ New system in 114): For children under 6 years old, 150,000 yuan for the first place, 225,000 yuan for each person after the second place (cancel the wealth exclusion clause)
  • Special deduction for long-term care (✅ New system in 2014): 180,000 yuan per person for eligible disabled persons (increased from the original 120,000 yuan, retroactive to January 1, 2014)
  • Special deduction for house rent (✅ Independent since 113): 180,000 yuan per household for owner-occupiers and renters

⚠️ There is still a lot of outdated information on the Internet (especially 120,000 for long-term care and 120,000 for preschool). Please be sure to use the above new figures, which shall be subject to the 2026 announcement of the Ministry of Finance.

It is particularly important to note that if you have a 6% self-tax withdrawal, this amount is deducted from your salary income and does not require income tax. A person with a monthly salary of 40,000 yuan can withdraw 2,400 yuan every month, which is 28,800 yuan tax-free in a year. If the tax rate is 5%, he can save 1,440 yuan. If the tax rate is 12%, he can save 3,456 yuan.

Step 5: Calculate estimated tax amount and plan in advance

Rather than waiting for the tax filing system to calculate it for you, it is better to estimate how much tax you will have to pay first. The calculation formula is roughly:

Net comprehensive income = Total income – Exemptions – Deductions – Special deductions

The tax exemption for 2025 is 97,000 yuan per person. Take a single office worker with a monthly salary of 45,000 yuan as an example: the annual income is about 540,000 yuan (including the year-end one month), after deducting the tax exemption of 97,000 yuan, the standard deduction amount of 131,000 yuan, and the special salary deduction amount of 218,000 yuan, the comprehensive net income is about 94,000 yuan, the applicable tax rate is 5%, and the tax payable is about 4,700 yuan.

If the estimated amount of tax to be paid is high, you can use the Date Calculator to calculate how many days are left until the tax payment deadline (6/1), and prepare funds for tax payment in advance to avoid being rushed. If the amount of tax payable** exceeds 30,000 yuan, you can apply for payment in up to 3 installments**, or pay in 0-12 installments by credit card (depending on bank discounts).

Step 6: Check if there are tax refund opportunities

Many office workers don’t know that they can actually get a tax refund. Common tax refund situations include:

  • The monthly income tax withheld by the company (the amount of withheld tax) exceeds the actual amount of tax you should pay** (most common)
  • There were large amounts of medical expenses last year, and the tax amount after deductions was reduced
  • There is a resignation window in the middle of the year, and the actual annual income is lower than expected
  • Dependent immediate relatives** who are over 70 years old** (the tax exemption is doubled to 145,500)
  • There were large donations last year (such as stock and real estate donations)

Your tax refund is usually credited to your bank account in batches between the end of July and January. Those who complete the online declaration before May 10th with no errors will be credited in the first batch (7/31); the second batch (10/31) for those who declare from 5/11-5/31; supplementary documents and manual reviewers will be credited at the latest on 1/20.

Step 7: Overview of tax filing schedule and precautions

Write down important dates so you don’t miss them:

  • Starting from April 28: Income and deduction information can be checked
  • May 1st to May 31st (extension to June 1st): Comprehensive income tax filing period
  • Tax payment method: Credit card (including 0-12 installments), transfer, convenience store (less than 20,000), ATM transfer, mobile payment scan QR Code

Here are some common pitfalls:

  • Couples can choose to file separately or jointly in the first year of marriage (after that they will all be jointly filed)
  • Dependent relatives must confirm that the other person has not been declared by others (each person can only be included by one declarer)
  • If renters want to list rent deductions, the landlord may have objections, but you have the right to declare according to the law
  • It is recommended to scan and archive the listed donation, insurance, and medical documents. The IRS may require replacement copies.

Step 8: Don’t step on these 5 minefields

Every year, the IRS publishes a ranking of errors. Among the mistakes that office workers are most likely to make:

  • Off-reporting of dividends or sideline income: Brokerage dividends, podcast advertising profit sharing, Shopee stores, tutoring, author fees, YouTube advertising, etc., the IRS has collected information from various platforms
  • Listed house rent without complete contract and proof of remittance (now changed to special deduction, but supporting documents are still required)
  • Parents reporting mutual support results in duplicate declarations for brothers and sisters: Each dependent relative can only be declared by one person
  • Check “Separate tax calculation for husband and wife” but the details are calculated incorrectly and you pay more: The tax filing software will automatically do a trial calculation, just choose the lowest
  • Wrong account number for tax refund account: If the remittance fails, you must apply again, and the account may be delayed for 1-2 months.

FAQ

Is it necessary to use a natural person certificate when filing taxes?

uncertain. In addition to the natural person certificate, you can also use your health insurance card (you must first register a password on the National Health Insurance Agency website), mobile natural person certificate (TW FidO mobile app), or mobile phone authentication to log in to the tax filing system. Among them, mobile phone verification is the most convenient. You only need the mobile phone number (under your name), health insurance card number and ID card number to complete the identity authentication.

How much annual income can I avoid paying tax?

Judging from the tax exemption threshold in 2025, if the annual salary of a single office worker does not exceed 446,000 yuan (tax exemption amount of 97,000 yuan + standard deduction amount of 131,000 yuan + special salary deduction amount of 218,000 yuan), he will basically not have to pay tax. But even if you don’t have to pay tax, if the company has withholding income tax, it is recommended that you still have to declare it to get the tax refund**.

Will I be fined if I forget to file my tax return?

According to Article 110 of the Income Tax Law, individuals who are required to declare but fail to declare and have tax payable will be fined not more than three times the amount of tax omitted in addition to the back tax; if they have declared but failed to report, they will be fined not more than two times the tax amount. However, if adequate reporting and payment are made automatically without reporting or investigation**, according to Article 48-1 of the Tax Collection Act, the person may be exempt from penalty and only need to accrue interest. A 1% late payment fee will be levied for every three days overdue tax payment, and enforcement will be enforced if the tax remains unpaid for more than 30 days. ⚠️ Note: The “10% late filing fee / 20% late filing fee” often quoted in popular articles is the profit-making enterprise income tax (company number) regulations, and individual comprehensive income tax does not apply.

How to choose the most cost-effective way for couples to file tax returns?

There are three tax calculation methods for couples: joint taxation of all income, separate taxation of salary income, and separate taxation of various types of income. Generally speaking, if the salary gap between the two parties is large, it is usually more advantageous to calculate taxes separately. The tax filing system will automatically help you calculate the results of the three methods, just choose the lowest one.

Do I have platform creation income (YouTube, Podcast, industry distribution) to report?

want. The Ministry of Finance will clearly include the income of Internet celebrities and platform content creators in the comprehensive income tax in 2026. The counseling period will be until June 30, 2026. During this period, there will be no penalty or interest for automatic supplementary filing and payment. If you have YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, TikTok advertising profit sharing, business matching income or paid subscriptions in 2025, be sure to make a supplementary report during the coaching period.

Filing taxes is not scary, the scary thing is going into it without preparation. While there are still a few weeks until May, prepare all the documents and calculate the deductions, and you can file your return in 20 minutes.
