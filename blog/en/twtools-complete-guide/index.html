Image processing: format conversion, compression, and resizing all at once

Let’s start with the most common needs. Making presentations, writing articles, and uploading to websites often require processing images.

The “Picture Tool” on this site supports the mutual conversion of four common formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF, and can also directly compress and adjust the size. The most practical thing is the batch processing function - you can upload 10 or 20 pictures at a time, convert or compress them uniformly, and then download the entire ZIP file with one click, saving you the trouble of processing them one by one.

The actual scenario is this: you have a bunch of screenshots in PNG format, but you want to upload them to a platform that only accepts JPGs. In the past, you might have to install Photoshop or GIMP, or find an online conversion tool to convert one by one. Now you just need to throw all the files into the image tool, set the output format, and convert them all at once, without even having to click on them one by one to download them.

⚠️ Attention for iPhone users: Uploading the default HEIC format to Taiwan e-commerce, government websites, and LINE OA backends is often rejected. You can use TWTools picture tool to convert to JPG with one click, or set “Camera → Format → Most Compatible (JPEG)” on iPhone to completely solve the problem.

PDF tools: merge, split, OCR, and sign in one go

The need to process PDF files is also common. Sometimes you have several PDFs that you want to combine into one for your supervisor, and sometimes you need to extract only a few pages from a 50-page document.

The “PDF Tool” on this site can help you:

  • Merge: Directly upload more than 2 PDFs and merge them into one in the order you choose.
  • Split: Select the desired page number range from a PDF to generate a new PDF file
  • Compression: Compress a 25MB large file to less than 5MB, passing the email attachment limit
  • OCR: Convert scanned “image PDF” into searchable, text-selectable PDF
  • Electronic Signature: Embed handwritten signature PNG or digital signature

For example, if you want to send pages 3 to 7 of the contract to the legal department for review, you don’t need to send the entire copy. You can directly use the split function to take out those pages. The file will be smaller and clearer**. Or if you have three PDFs of employee signature page, confidentiality agreement, and salary slip, and need to be bound into one, just merge them directly.

Daily inquiries: exchange rates, units, lottery tickets, suspension of classes and classes

Then there are some “query-type” tools. These tools may not be used frequently, but they are very practical at critical moments.

Exchange rate conversion

Provides real-time exchange rates, enter the amount and see the conversion results immediately. Supports 30+ currencies (USD, Japanese Yen, Euro, Korean Won, RMB, Singapore Dollar, Malaysian Ringgit, Thai Baht, etc.). Traveling abroad, making international transfers, checking US stock prices, and comparing prices before swiping overseas credit cards are much faster than searching through Google search results.

Unit conversion

Supports kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, and degrees Celsius to Fahrenheit. Especially when reading foreign recipes or technical documents, it is very annoying that the units are not unified. It is really convenient to have a tool at hand. You can also quickly calculate electricity bills in degrees, wattage, and kilowatt-hour conversion.

Typhoon suspension of classes and classes inquiry

It is a demand unique to Taiwan. During the typhoon season, the biggest worry every day is whether companies or schools will be closed. This tool real-timely displays the status of 22 counties and cities in Taiwan, saving you the time of checking government announcements one by one.

Taiwan Lottery

You can check the latest lottery numbers of Power Lottery, Big Lotto, and Double Win Lottery, as well as historical lottery statistics.

Time tools: date calculation and countdown

The “Date Calculator” on this site is easier to use than imagined. you can:

  • Calculate the difference between two dates
  • Add or subtract days from a certain date (for example, “Today is May 15th, what day is it if you add 30 days?”)
  • Calculate working days (real working days excluding holidays)

Common scenarios are “how many days are left between the contract date and the delivery deadline”, “calculating the end date of the employee’s probation period” and “project progress outlook”. Manual calculations are prone to errors, and you will have the answer as soon as you lose.

“Countdown Timer” supports preset holidays such as New Year countdown and birthday countdown, and you can also customize the date. For example, personal goals such as “15 days left before traveling abroad” and “N days left until the 2026 Spring Festival holiday”. Browser notifications let you never miss important moments.

Work essentials: resume generator and typo checker

If you are looking for a job recently or need to help colleagues review their resumes, these two tools will be very useful.

Resume generator

Provides 3 templates and 8 color combinations, allowing you to quickly create a professional-looking resume. What’s even more considerate is that it has built-in AI retouching function - enter your work experience and achievements, and AI will help you rewrite it into a more convincing narrative. You can also generate an autobiography, saving you the time of trying to introduce yourself from scratch. After completion, you can directly export the PDF, the format is neat and beautiful, and the design is friendly through ATS screening.

⚠️ In 2026, 85% of Taiwan’s top 100 companies have introduced AI resume screening (ATS). The design of the resume tool on this site conforms to ATS-friendly format (single column, standard font, full copy without image format).

Typo check

Using AI to proofread Chinese can catch common mistakes, such as:

  • “Related” is written as “want to close”
  • “Appropriate” is written as “appropriate amount”
  • Wrong usage of “real-time” vs “timely”
  • Inconsistent full/half-width symbols

When writing resumes, reports, and emails, one typo may ruin the professionalism of the entire document. Paste the text and immediately give correction suggestions.

For those who don’t have time to prepare an itinerary and don’t know where to go, this tool can really help you get started quickly.

FAQ Q&A

Q1: Are these tools really free? Are there any usage restrictions? A: Completely free, no login requirements, and no annoying paywalls. AI-related functions (resume polishing, typo checking, TripCraft) have a daily usage limit, but they are more than enough for daily use.

Q2: Will the uploaded files be saved? Is the privacy safe? A: Most tools (image processing, PDF operations, unit conversion, etc.) are operated locally in the browser, and the files will not be transferred to any server. Only the AI ​​function will send the text for processing through an encrypted connection. It will be deleted after processing and will not be retained. For details, please view the privacy policy of this site.

**Q3: Can it also be used on mobile phones? ** A: Yes. TWTools has a responsive design and can be used by opening it in a mobile browser. Although some tools will be better experienced on a larger screen (such as the editing interface of the resume generator), the basic functions can operate normally.

Q4: Is there an API provided? A: There is currently no public API. If you have business integration needs (for example, you want to integrate exchange rate queries into your own system), you can contact the TWTools team to discuss licensing options.

Q5: Will the tool be continuously updated? A: Yes. We fix bugs and add new features every month based on user feedback. Major updates will be announced in the “Knowledge Articles” area. For example, in 2026, AVIF image format support, PDF OCR Chinese recognition, TripCraft 30+ currency automatic detection, etc. will be added.

Q6: What tools can be added? Is there a proposal pipeline? A: Yes. You can click “Contact Us” on the TWTools homepage to report your needs, or recommend your commonly used tools on the Aire contribution page. User proposals will be included in the next round of development priority.
