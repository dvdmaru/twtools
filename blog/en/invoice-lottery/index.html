Taiwan Uniform Invoice Lottery 2026: Schedule, Prize Tiers & Cloud Invoice Auto-Claim

Life Tips · February 22, 2026 · 10 min

The unified invoice is a unique tax system design in Taiwan. Through the “Invoice Match”, a national activity, consumers are encouraged to request invoices from merchants, indirectly assisting the government in tax audits. The lottery is held every two months with a special prize of up to NT$10 million. However, many people are not familiar with the prize collection channels, the advantages of automatic remittance of cloud invoices, and the “3 months from the 6th of the next month” rule for the prize redemption period. This article clarifies it once and for all.

Why does Taiwan have invoice matching? Institutional origins

The unified invoice prize system began in 1951 and was designed by the Ministry of Finance at the time. The purpose was not a lottery but a tax audit. The principle is:

  • If a merchant does not issue an invoice, it is equivalent to underreporting business tax.
  • Consumers must ask for an invoice in order to claim the prize, so they will naturally “ask for an invoice”
  • Merchants are forced to issue invoices → the government captures the full turnover → the tax base expands

This design has been in operation for more than 70 years and is one of the most successful audit tools for Taiwan taxation. Until now, “Have you given an invoice?” is still the most common question consumers ask when facing small stores.

In recent years, due to the popularity of cloud invoices, the system has been expanded from paper to “vehicles” (mobile phone barcodes, Easy Cards, credit card binding, etc.), making invoice matching more convenient, and automatic remittances can be exempted from stamp tax.

Lottery draw schedule: 6 draws per year, on the 25th of odd months

The unified invoice is issued every two months, 6 issues in total in a year, and the lottery draw date is fixed on the 25th of every odd-numbered month**:

The lottery numbers will be announced by the Ministry of Finance in the afternoon of the lottery day, and the prizes can be matched from that day on.

Overall, the winning probability of any invoice is about 9.4% (mainly contributed by the six prizes and the additional six prizes), which means that about 1 out of every 10 invoices can win 200 yuan.

Please note: The prize collection pipeline is not the IRS (many older articles incorrectly stated this). The IRS is responsible for “re-issuance of invoices and dispute adjudication” and is not responsible for cash redemption**.

Redemption period: 3 months from the 6th of the following month

This is the most misunderstood part. The prize collection period is not “6 months from the draw date”, the correct rule is:

  • Starting date: The 6th of the next month** of the lottery month**
  • Deadline: 3 months after the start date (including the current day)

Example (lottery draw on January 25, 2026, invoice for November-December):

  • Starting date: February 6, 2026
  • Deadline: May 5, 2026 (within 3 months)
  • If there is a holiday that year, the deadline will be postponed to the next working day

After the deadline, ** overdue will be regarded as abandoned and no reissue will be given**. In each issue, lottery tickets expired because the holder forgot to claim them, and the annual total of nearly 10 million yuan in prizes was lost.

Paper prize matching vs cloud invoice: automatic remittance is a hidden advantage

Paper invoice award process

  1. Obtain the invoice and confirm that it is complete and not torn or altered**
  2. Compare the lottery numbers after the lottery draw
  3. The winner brings the invoice and ID card to the prize redemption channel
  4. For prizes above 4,000 yuan, a stamp tax of 4/1,000 will be deducted (for 4,000 yuan, 16 yuan will be deducted) + 20% income tax (for 4,000 yuan, 800 yuan will be deducted)
  5. Actual amount received: 4,000 - 16 - 800 = 3,184 yuan

Cloud invoice matching process (recommended)

  1. Set Mobile Barcode (Registration on the Ministry of Finance’s Electronic Invoice Integration Service Platform)
  2. Inform the store of “vehicle” when making a purchase, and the invoice will be returned to your home electronically.
  3. Automatically match the prize after the lottery is drawn, and automatically send an email/app notification if you win the prize
  4. For those who have bound a bank account: The bonus will be automatically transferred into your account
  5. No Stamp Duty Deduction (Cloud invoice has no paper copy, stamp tax is 16 yuan free) + 20% income tax is also deducted
  6. The cloud invoice of 4,000 yuan is actually credited to the account: 4,000 - 800 = 3,200 yuan

Hidden advantages of cloud invoicing

In addition to saving stamp duty, the period limit for cloud invoices is 5 issues (about 10 months), instead of the paper “3 months from the 6th of the next month”. Winning prizes will not expire because of “forgetting to claim them”. For those who often buy low-consumption invoices, the accumulated winnings in 5 periods can be remitted in one go, saving you the trouble of redeeming your prizes multiple times.

Steps to set mobile phone barcode (recommended for everyone):

  1. Go to [MoF Electronic Invoice Integration Service Platform] (https://www.einvoice.nat.gov.tw) (einvoice.nat.gov.tw) to apply for a mobile phone barcode
  2. Enter “Host Account Settings” to bind a bank account (any Taiwan bank account)
  3. After completing the purchase, please ask the store to scan the barcode on your mobile phone.
  4. Winning prizes will be automatically transferred to the bound account.

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions about Invoice Verification

Q1: What does it mean when the words “No Winning Invoice” are written on the invoice?

Some merchants will print a non-winning mark when issuing “Free Unified Invoice” or “Electronic Computer Invoice”. This type of invoice cannot be matched for prizes and is more common in small transaction areas such as vendors, self-service laundries, and parking lots. Generally, “cash register unified invoice” (those with the words “unified invoice” printed in blue, purple or white) can win prizes.

Q2: Can I redeem the prize if the invoice is blurred or damaged?

As long as the prize number digits are clearly legible and the invoice body is complete, the prize can still be redeemed. However, if any of the last 8 digits is so blurred that it cannot be recognized, it is recommended to apply for a photocopy of the invoice from the IRS for confirmation before proceeding accordingly. It is recommended that you take photos and save the invoice immediately after receiving the invoice as a backup.

Q3: How to match the invoice for credit card payment?

A unified invoice will still be issued for credit card swiping. If it is a paper invoice (such as a restaurant, mass merchandiser), the bill and invoice are separated, and the invoice must still be verified by itself. If you use Credit Card Transfer (some card-issuing banks have this service), the invoice will be directly electronic and will not be printed on paper. It will be automatically compared with the cloud invoice. It is recommended to check whether this function is available in the card-issuing bank’s app.

Q4: Are there invoices for overseas online purchases (such as Amazon and Taobao)?

No. The unified invoice is a unique system in Taiwan and is not covered by overseas e-commerce companies. However, **overseas e-commerce companies will issue electronic invoices through Taiwan tax agents (such as Apple, Google, and Netflix subscriptions), and the rewards can be returned to your home through the vehicle.

Q5: What should I do if the merchant doesn’t give me an invoice?

According to the “Value-Added and Non-Value-Added Business Tax Law”, merchants who refuse to issue invoices can report to the Ministry of Finance and the National Taxation Bureau. If the report is successful, there will be a bonus (5-20% of the tax evasion amount). Reporting channels: Dial 0800-000-321 (the service hotline of the National Taxation Bureau) or go to the Ministry of Finance Tax Portal to report online.

Conclusion: paper skills, cloud, etc.

The best strategy for unified invoice matching:

  1. Set mobile phone barcode: Use vehicles as much as possible for all purchases, save stamp duty + no need to run to redeem prizes + will not expire
  2. A few paper invoices: Prizes will be matched immediately after the lottery day. For amounts above 4,000 yuan, go to the bank/credit union for processing.
  3. Avoid expiration: Paper period is “3 months from the 6th of the next month”, cloud period 5 is about 10 months
  4. Keep all invoices for at least one month: for financial and tax records, return and exchange vouchers
