2026 Taiwan Public Holidays Complete Guide: 9 Long Weekends & Optimal Leave Strategy

Life Tips · March 8, 2026 · 8 min

Why can some people use a small amount of leave to create extremely long consecutive vacations, while others have their vacations broken up every time? The secret lies in planning in advance + mastering the two new systems in 2026: No make-up days throughout the year (the make-up system will be abolished from the second half of 2025) + Five new national holidays (Labor Day/Confucius’ Birthday/Taiwan Liberation Day/Constitution Day/New Year’s Eve). This article compiles the complete 2026 calendar + leave guide, and teaches you how to create the longest vacation with the least special leave.

Two new systems for 2026 Taiwan calendar

1. Cancel the make-up system (from the second half of 2025)

In the past, when national holidays fell on Saturdays or Sundays, the central government would announce “compensatory work days” (working on Saturdays would make up for a weekday). Starting from the second half of 2025, only make-up days will be provided but no make-up days - that is to say, there will be no make-up days in 2026. Good news for office workers: There will no longer be the weird situation of “having to work a make-up day on Saturday just to have four consecutive days off”.

2. Added 5 new national holidays (the “Regulations on the Implementation of Memorial Days and Holidays” came into effect on 2025-05-28)

In the past, Labor Day, the abolished Taiwan Liberation Day and other holidays that were only reserved for workers for many years, will all return to national holidays in 2025 after the Legislative Yuan amended the law**:

Strategy for asking for consecutive holidays: Use the least special leave to create the longest holiday

The strongest combination: Spring Festival + 228, 4 consecutive days off, 16 days of fun

If you want to extend the Spring Festival holiday, you can combine the Spring Festival (2/14-2/22) and the 228 consecutive holidays (2/27-3/1) into a 16-day super long holiday (2/14 ~ 3/1) by taking 4 days of special leave from 2/23 (Monday) to 2/26 (Thursday). Here are the highest ROI leave strategies for 2026.

Suggestions for leave:

  • Arrange travel bookings early: Air tickets and accommodation prices will increase by 30-50% during the Spring Festival, and 16-day itineraries need to be booked 6 months in advance.
  • Avoid Friday eve of 2/13: the largest number of people returning to their hometowns, so you can leave in the early morning of 2/14 instead

Spring Festival: 9 days of consecutive holidays

Official holiday: 2/14 (Saturday) ~ 2/22 (Sunday), a total of 9 days, naturally formed with the weekends before and after.

Tips for asking for leave

Submit leave application early

Most companies require vacation requests to be submitted 2-4 weeks in advance. Competition for popular holidays is fierce:

  • Spring Festival Holiday: Submit in December at the latest (high outbreak of return to hometown)
  • Qingming Festival: Submit no later than February
  • Summer vacation: Submit no later than May

Submitting early not only has a higher success rate, but can also help the company make personnel adjustments.

Make the most of your work-from-home day (WFH)

If the company allows WFH:

  • Leave + WFH combination: Please take 4/2 (Thursday) but can WFH for half a day, so you can spend time with your family and keep working
  • Every other week WFH: If you cannot apply for consecutive leave, you can consider taking 1-2 days off + WFH on other dates

Avoid booking during peak hours

  • Spring Festival: Avoid the core period of 2/14-2/22, instead go to 2/23-2/27, which is also a holiday but with half the number of people.
  • Qingming Tomb Sweeping: If it is not necessary to sweep the tomb, use alternative dates such as 4/2-4/6
  • Summer vacation: There are fewer people in mid-July than in early July, and air tickets are 20-30% cheaper

Vacation allowance (if allowed by the company system)

Some companies allow employees to accumulate annual leave, for example at the end of the year:

  • Do not waste this year’s vacation, accumulate it to the next year or flexible date
  • Properly plan a multi-year long-term vacation plan (for example, accumulate 2-3 years of vacation and go abroad for 1 month at a time)

FAQ

Q1: Is there really no make-up day in 2026? A:Yes. After the Legislative Yuan amends the law in the second half of 2025, it will only make up for vacations but not work hours**. Therefore, all holidays caused by national holidays in 2026 are “earned” rest days, and there is no need to work on Saturdays to pay them back.

Q2:5/1 Can civil servants take a holiday on Labor Day? A: Yes! Starting from 2025-05-28, the “Regulations on the Implementation of Memorial Days and Holidays” has added Labor Day as a national holiday. Civil servants, faculty, and students will all have a holiday on May 1 from 2026. However, the actual holidays for police, fire fighters, coast patrol, and military personnel may be moved due to their shift duties.

Q3: How to calculate the 9 consecutive days of holidays during the Spring Festival? A: New Year’s Eve 2/15 (Sunday) + New Year’s Eve 2/16 (Monday) + Spring Festival 2/17-2/19 (Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday) + 2/20 (Friday) compensatory leave + 2/21-2/22 (weekend) = 9 days in total (2/14-2/22 including the previous Saturday).

Q4:228 Is it true that the holidays and the Spring Festival are 16 days in series? A:Yes. 2/14-2/22 Spring Festival 9 days + 2/23-2/26 please take 4 days of special leave + 2/27-3/1 228 3 consecutive days of leave = 16 consecutive days. is the highest ROI leave strategy in 2026.

Q5: Why is the Mid-Autumn Festival on 9/25 instead of some day at the end of September? A: The Mid-Autumn Festival falls on 8/15 in the lunar calendar. In 2026, 8/15 in the lunar calendar corresponds to 9/25 (Friday) in the national calendar. Naturally, it is combined with the weekend + 9/28 (Monday) Teacher’s Day to form a four-day holiday.
