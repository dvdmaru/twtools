Your After-Work Side Hustle: Essential Free Online Tools for Freelancing

Productivity · April 7, 2026 · 8 min

Starting a new side income stream after get off work sounds tempting, but it often comes with new challenges. In addition to completing cases within a limited time, they also have to face various work processes - client communication, document management, financial calculations, file processing - each of which requires corresponding tools. And for those who are starting down the slash path, the subscription fees for tools are the first hidden expense.

I have encountered many pitfalls in the past few years when I have been taking on cases. At first, I foolishly paid for several software subscriptions, but later I discovered that many free online tools can solve most problems. Today I will share the free tools that have made my case-taking life much easier, and how to use them to reduce costs and improve efficiency.

Image batch processing: a daily savior for designers and community editors

If your side business involves visual content—whether it’s case design, community management, or handicraft seller—image processing should be a weekly routine. I often encounter this scenario: the customer requires 20 product images to be compressed and sent to him in a package, or materials of different proportions need to be converted into specific sizes and uploaded to various platforms.

In the past, I would use Photoshop one by one, which was time-consuming and labor-intensive. Later, I discovered that some free tools can batch compress, convert formats, and resize, which directly saves a lot of time. The “Picture Tool” on this site can process multiple photos at once, supports conversion between JPG, PNG, and WebP formats, and the quality will not be reduced too much after compression. I usually run through the pictures before sending them to clients or uploading them to the community.

The benefit of this tool is not only to save time, but also to help you improve your professionalism invisibly - customers receive the most optimized materials and the loading speed is fast, which invisibly improves the quality of your service.

Exchange rate conversion for freelancers: essential homework when taking on overseas cases

After working on the side for a while, you may start taking cases from overseas clients. I felt very excited at first, but then came a practical problem: How to convert the quotation into the local currency? The exchange rate changes every day, and you may lose a lot if you make a mistake.

I have tried searching for real-time exchange rates on Google and then calculating them manually, but the calculations often resulted in errors or wasted time. Now I use the “Exchange Rate Conversion” tool on this site to quickly check the real-time exchange rates of major currencies - US dollars, euros, Japanese yen, RMB, and of course the Taiwan dollar.

When I need to calculate the income from overseas cases at the end of the month, I am also accustomed to converting all quotes into Taiwan dollars and using the same currency unit to calculate clearly. In this way, you can not only grasp the actual income, but also feel more confident when making quotations to customers.

Document organization: systematic management of contracts, portfolios, and invoices

People who take on cases should all have a common problem: How to manage more and more contracts, proposal documents, and invoices? Especially when there are many cases, it often takes a long time to look through old contracts or proposal templates.

My approach is to organize it regularly, merging, splitting, and renaming related PDF files. For example, I will combine all the invoices of a certain month into one for easy reference when filing taxes; or a client may request that multiple proposal documents be bound into one file. The “PDF Tool” on this site has merge and split functions, it is completely free, no login is required, and privacy is guaranteed - your files will not be uploaded to the server. I usually take some time to sort it out after get off work, and I can summarize the week’s documents in ten minutes.

Resume and profile optimization: stand out on the case-taking platform

The first step in accepting a case is often to create a personal profile on the platform. Whether it is Upwork, Fiverr or an outsourcing platform in Taiwan, your personal introduction and portfolio directly affect the quality and quantity of cases you can receive.

My own approach is to first write an “About Me” introduction in a document - listing my skills, experience, and works, and then adjust it according to the needs of different platforms. Here’s a little trick: Use the “Resume Generator” on this site to first generate a basic framework, and then fine-tune it according to your own content. It has an AI polishing function that can help you write your self-introduction more convincingly. This does not mean that you must use a generator, but it can help you quickly organize your thoughts and ensure that your resume covers key information.

Many people underestimate the importance of the step of “introducing yourself.” A clear and organized personal profile will attract more suitable customers to come to you.

Copywriting quality control: Don’t let typos ruin your professional image

This lesson is a lesson of blood and tears. One time, I didn’t check carefully when making a proposal. As a result, there were typos in the copy, and the client pointed them out on the spot. Although I finally received the case, I decided at that moment that I would never make this mistake again.

Now, whenever there is any external copywriting—proposal letters, product introductions, community posts—I will run it through the “typo check” on this site. It can not only find obvious typos, but also catch some confusing vocabulary usage. Although this small action only takes three to five minutes, it can greatly improve your professionalism in the eyes of customers.

Some typos are hard to spot with your eyes, especially when you’re in a hurry. Relying on tools to assist inspection is a sign of respect for customers and yourself.

Time management: Countdown timer and date calculation help you master every deadline

The biggest fear of side hustle is losing control of time management. Your regular job already takes up eight hours, and you have to deal with multiple cases at the same time after get off work, so it’s easy to miss a deadline.

My approach is to set the deadline for each case on the “countdown timer” on this site. I set it up as soon as I receive the case, and when the countdown reaches the last 48 hours, I speed up the work. The visual countdown will make the passage of time more “real” - you will not relax just because there is one week left, but will instead plan your work more actively.

When agreeing on a delivery date with a customer, I will also use the “Date Calculator” to accurately calculate the number of working days. For example, if the customer says “delivery within 10 working days,” I will calculate the specific date and check whether there are consecutive holidays during the period. Taiwan has a lot of holidays, especially the Spring Festival and consecutive holidays. If you don’t think about it in advance, it is easy to promise a deadline that cannot be met.

Side business tax 2026: 200,000 threshold + new system for small-scale business persons

The most common pitfall when taking on a side business is not the tools, but taxes. There are two figures worthy of special attention in the side business tax system in 2026: The business tax threshold of 200,000 and The threshold for small-scale business owners to be increased starting from 2025-01.

Two independent tax systems

Personal Comprehensive Income Tax (Annual Settlement): Side business income belongs to “Income from Executing Business” (code 9A or 50) or “Other Income” (Code 9B) and needs to be incorporated into the personal comprehensive income tax return in May of the following year. The tax-free income threshold for single office workers in 2026 is NT$464,000 (total tax exemption, deductions, and salary deductions).

Business tax (monthly/quarterly, depending on the scale): This is another independent system. As long as your side business is sustainable and profitable, it may be deemed as “sales of services/sales of goods” and requires registration and taxation.

Small-scale business operators: 2025-01 New three-stage system

Starting from 2025-01-01, the threshold for small-scale business operators will be raised:

How to deal with three common situations in side business

  1. Monthly case-taking income < 50,000 (common side business): No tax registration is required. You only need to declare “business income” in the annual comprehensive income tax.
  2. Continuously receiving cases of 50,000-200,000 yuan per month (growth period): It is recommended to actively apply for tax registration For small-scale business operators, the 1% business tax will be assessed and levied by the National Taxation Bureau every quarter. Failure to register proactively and being caught with back taxes + fines are more risky.
  3. More than 200,000 per month (professional case acceptance): Must apply for the use of unified invoice + monthly business tax of 5%. It is recommended to contact an accountant to handle it.

Decision tree: When you are not sure whether to register, directly call 0800-000-321 (free tax consultation from the Ministry of Finance) to ask about your case. There is no charge and there is confidentiality, which is much better than being caught and fined afterwards.

FAQ

**Q: Will there be any concerns about information security when using free tools? **

A: When choosing tools, give priority to those platforms that do not require login, files are processed locally in the browser (not uploaded to the server), and have clear privacy policies. Be especially cautious if sensitive information is involved (such as customer lists or quotation figures). Most of TWTools’ tools are front-end operations and will not send your files out.

**Q: What is the difference between paid tools and free tools? **

A: Free tools are sufficient for 80% of daily needs. Paid tools often come with advanced features, increased batch processing limits, or customer support. But for a side hustle just starting out, these are often not necessary. When your side business really grows to a certain scale, you can then evaluate whether it needs to be upgraded.

**Q: Is there a recommended order for using these tools? **

A: Start with the most commonly used ones. If your side business involves visual content, equip yourself with image processing tools first; if you take on overseas cases, familiarize yourself with exchange rate tools first; if there are many cases, get time management tools first. Tools are there to serve your workflow, not the other way around.

The road to side hustle doesn’t have to be expensive. Some seemingly advanced work processes will actually make your work more systematic and professional with the right tools. These free tools are like laying a solid foundation for your side business, allowing you to focus on the most important thing - completing client cases and accumulating your strength and reputation.
