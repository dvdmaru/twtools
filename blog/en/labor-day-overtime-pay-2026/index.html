2026 Taiwan Labor Day: First Year of Nationwide Holiday — Overtime Pay 2× Wages, Comp Time, and Complaint Channels

Labor Rights · April 26, 2026 · 10 min

Labor Day, May 1, is quickly approaching again. 2026 is a historic year: After the amendment of the “Regulations on the Implementation of Memorial Days and Holidays” on 2025-05-28, May 1st Labor Day** was included in the national national holiday** for the first time, and all civil servants, faculty and students were given a day off (in the past, only laborers were given a day off). This article summarizes the applicable objects of Labor Day, the 2 times wage formula for overtime pay, the actual trial calculation of a monthly salary of 30,000 to 60,000, the 1:1 provision for compensatory time off, as well as the appeal channels and penalties when the employer violates the law. The information in this article is updated as of April 2026.

2026 New Labor Day System: From “Labor Exclusive” to “National Uniform”

Law revision background

In the past many years, only “workers” protected by Article 37 of the Labor Standards Act could have a holiday on May 1st. Students, teachers, civil servants and other non-worker people still have to go to work on May 1st - creating a strange phenomenon of “parents have a holiday but their children have to go to school” and “the husband has a holiday but his wife is a civil servant and has to go to work”.

On May 28, 2025, the Legislative Yuan passed the “Regulations on the Implementation of Memorial Days and Holidays”, adding 5 new national holidays: New Year’s Eve / 5/1 Labor Day / 9/28 Confucius’s Birthday / 10/25 Taiwan Liberation Day / 12/25 Constitution Anniversary. The new law will take full effect from 2026.

Applicable objects for Labor Day on May 1, 2026

  • National holiday: labor, civil servants, faculty and students (unified for the first time nationwide)
  • Special Transfer: Police, fire fighters, coast guard, and military personnel may be transferred to other days for compensatory leave due to “year-round shift duty”, and may not necessarily take the rest on 5/1
  • Financial Market: Domestic bank employees are on holiday, but financial markets are still open (US and Taiwan stock exchanges have their own closing rules)
  • Garbage Truck: According to the announcement of each county and city environmental protection bureau, usually no collection on 5/1

May 1, 2026 is Friday, which together with Saturday (5/2) and Sunday (5/3) form a 3-day holiday (the first time there are 3 days of holidays nationwide).

How to calculate overtime pay for Labor Day attendance? Breakdown of 2 times salary formula

Article 39 of the Labor Standards Law stipulates that if the employer works on a holiday with the employee’s consent, ** wages shall be doubled to **. This “double” means “in addition to the original day’s salary, plus one day’s salary”, so you actually get 2 times the daily salary.

Calculation formula

  • Monthly Salary System: Daily Salary = Monthly Salary ÷ 30
  • Labor Day attendance fee = monthly salary ÷ 30 × 1 (originally included in monthly salary) + monthly salary ÷ 30 × 1 (additional)
  • Actual extra amount received = Monthly salary ÷ 30

Note: If the work exceeds 8 hours, the excess will be added to 1.34 times or 1.67 times of the extended working hours on weekdays, not 2 times for the whole day.

Extended calculation for overtime work exceeding 8 hours

If overtime work reaches 10 hours on the same day, extended working hours overtime pay will be added to the additional 2 hours. Take monthly salary 40,000 as an example:

  • Hourly wage is about 167 yuan (40,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8)
  • First 8 hours: additional payment of RMB 1,333 (the “additional part” of 2 times salary)
  • Delay bonus in the 9th to 10th hour 1.34 times = 167 × 1.34 × 2 = About 447 yuan
  • Total should be extra = 1,333 + 447 = About 1,780 yuan

If you work overtime to 12 hours (the portion over 10 hours will be 1.67 times):

  • Hours 9-10: 1.34 × 2 hours = 447 yuan
  • Hour 11-12: 1.67 × 2 hours = 558 yuan
  • Total should be extra = 1,333 + 447 + 558 = About 2,338 yuan

⚠️ Exceeding 12 hours is prohibited: The maximum working hours in a single day is 12 hours (including normal working hours), and it is illegal for the employer to force overtime.

Can the boss require compensatory time off without overtime pay?

No, this is a common misunderstanding. According to Article 32-1 of the Labor Standards Act:

  • Compensatory leave must be chosen by the worker himself. Employers are not allowed to force compensatory leave to replace overtime pay.
  • Process: The employer first pays double wages in accordance with the law → If the employee wishes to take compensatory leave afterwards, both parties can negotiate
  • The standard of compensatory leave is “1 to 1”, not “1 to 2” (that is, after working 8 hours, you will make up 8 hours, not 16 hours)
  • Compensatory leave should be made up within the agreed period. If it is not made up, it will still be converted into salary and paid.

A common dispute in practice is when the boss says, “Our company always provides compensatory time off,” which is a violation of the Labor Standards Act. Workers have the right to ask for additional cash payments, and if they are faced with difficulties, they can appeal afterwards.

Regulations on making up work during the May Day holiday are inconsistent with asking for leave

May 1, 2026 is a Friday, there is no problem of making up shifts (the make-up shift system will be canceled from the second half of 2025). However, some companies will “voluntarily” require working on Saturdays. This situation is a “transfer” holiday in the labor agreement and must comply with:

  • Agree with the trade union or labor-management meeting
  • Prior announcement
  • After transfer, you must still comply with the 7-day holiday period (at least 1 day of regular leave within 7 days)

If you want to take a special day off on Thursday (4/30) and take five consecutive days off to go abroad, I would like to remind you to pay attention to the following points:

  • Special leave must be applied for in advance. Employers can only negotiate with you based on business operation needs and cannot refuse without reason.
  • If the leave period falls on Labor Day, 5/1 will not be counted as special leave and will not be deducted as special leave
  • When preparing for a travel trip, you can use the countdown timer to confirm how many days are left during the vacation and arrange air tickets and accommodation.

What should I do if my employer violates the law and refuses to pay overtime? 4-stage appeal process

If your boss refuses to pay overtime for Labor Day, you can take the following actions:

Phase 1: Preserve evidence (execute immediately)

  • Payslip (shows no additional payment)
  • Attendance records (punch records, sign-in sheets)
  • LINE/Email conversation (the boss said “just make up for the time off” and “the company doesn’t have double time”)
  • Screenshots of check-in and attendance management system

Phase 2: Internal Negotiation (Least Cost)

  • Write a letter or ask the company in person to reissue it in accordance with the law
  • Quoting Article 39 of the Labor Standards Act
  • Give the company 1-2 weeks to respond

Stage 3: Appeal channel (free)

  • Labor Bureau Report: Submit a report to the Labor Bureau where the company is located (County and Municipal Government Labor Bureau)
  • National toll-free number 1955 Labor consultation and complaint hotline (The identity of the whistleblower is kept confidential in accordance with the law)
  • Online Complaint: e-system of the Institute of Labor and Occupational Safety and Health of the Ministry of Labor

Stage 4: Mediation or Litigation

  • Labor dispute mediation: Apply to the local labor bureau (free, usually processed within 30-45 days)
  • Mediation fails → Labor Court Litigation (You can apply for a free lawyer from the Legal Aid Foundation)

Employer Penalties

Employers who violate Article 39 of the Labor Standards Law may be fined 20,000 to 1 million, and the name of the public institution and the name of the person in charge shall be published. The identity of the whistleblower is kept confidential in accordance with the law, and will not be fired in retaliation (if fired in retaliation, it is considered “unfair dismissal” and can be appealed again).

Cross-border 5/1 Comparison: Different positioning of the United States, Japan and South Korea

5/1 has different historical context and positioning in various countries around the world, and it is not a national holiday everywhere. Taiwan’s transformation from “labor exclusive” to “national unity” in 2026 is actually a new milestone in line with the spirit of “International Labor Day”.

United States: 5/1 is not Labor Day

Many people think that “May Day” is internationally unified, but Labor Day in the United States is the first Monday in September (legislated in 1894) - the date was deliberately moved away from May 1 in order to avoid commemorating the 1886 Chicago Haymarket Riots (this event is the historical origin of International Labor Day on May 1). Instead, 5/1 in the United States is designated as Loyalty Day, which is legal but not a public holiday. Most people will not pay special attention to it.

Japan: 5/1 is part of Golden Week

May 1st in Japan is not a national holiday, but because it is sandwiched between Showa Day on 4/29 + Constitution Memorial Day on 5/3 + Green Day on 5/4 + Children’s Day on 5/5, most companies will regard this day as a special holiday, allowing employees to enjoy the “Golden Week” for 7-9 consecutive days. Japanese workers mostly spend this day to travel abroad/domestic** rather than for trade union gatherings - this is a different atmosphere from the Labor Day in Europe and Taiwan.

South Korea: March 2026 has just been upgraded to a public holiday

Since 1958, Korea’s Labor Day (근로자의 날) has been a special position of “private sector paid leave + civil servants working”. Until March 2026, the South Korean Congress passed an amendment to raise 5/1 to a national holiday, and all civil servants, teachers, and students will have a holiday. This change is close to the timetable of Taiwan’s 2025-05-28 law amendment, reflecting the common trend of East Asian countries in recent years to “universalize” Labor Day.

FAQ

Q1: I am a monthly salary employee, will my salary be deducted during the Labor Day holiday? A: No. Labor Day is a “paid holiday”. Employees on a monthly salary system will be paid as they take this day off, and it does not count as absence from work. If the company deducts your salary because you didn’t show up for work, it’s a violation of the Labor Standards Act.

Q2: Are dispatched employees and part-time workers paid during Labor Day? A: Yes. As long as the Labor Standards Act applies, regardless of dispatch, part-time, or hourly workers, workers should be paid on Labor Day. For part-time workers, wages are calculated on a pro-rata basis. For example, if you agree to work 4 hours a day and have a holiday on that day, you should be paid 4 hours’ wages.

Q3: Labor Day happens to be my “regular holiday” (such as Saturday), can it be combined with the holiday? A: cannot be combined with false. If Labor Day overlaps with a regular holiday or a rest day, according to the Ministry of Labor’s explanation, you should “choose a compensatory day off.” Most employers will choose another day to make up for the holiday afterwards. It is recommended to confirm the date of compensatory leave with the company first.

Q4: I am an employee on probation, will I be free on Labor Day? A: Yes. The Labor Standards Law still applies to workers during the probation period, and the rights and interests on all national holidays are exactly the same. Employers are not allowed to give discounts on the grounds of “no leave during the probation period”, otherwise it is illegal.

Q5: Can civil servants really release 5/1 in 2026? A: It’s really possible. From 2025-05-28, the “Regulations on the Implementation of Remembrance Days and Holidays” has added Labor Day as a national holiday. Civil servants, faculty, and students will all have a holiday on May 1, 2026. This is the first time that Taiwan has held a national holiday for three days. However, the actual holidays for police, fire fighters, coast guard, and military personnel may be moved to other days due to their shift duties.

Q6: Are there Labor Day holidays for foreign migrant workers? A: Yes. The leave provisions of the Labor Standards Law also apply to home care workers and domestic helpers, and employers must pay them wages or double their wages in accordance with the law. If there is any violation, you can appeal to 1955 (supports multiple languages).
