How Much Emergency Fund Do You Need? 2026 Calculation Formula + Best High-Yield Savings for NT$30K-60K Workers

Personal Finance · April 19, 2026 · 10 min

With a monthly salary of RMB 30,000 to RMB 60,000, if your mobile phone suddenly breaks, your motorcycle is sent for repair, a family member gets sick and is hospitalized, or your company suddenly lays off employees…these things are not about “if it will happen” but “when”. The emergency fund is life-saving money that allows you to save money in these emergencies without having to swipe your card to borrow money or talk to your family.

This article uses the latest actual figures in 2026 to tell you: how much to save, where to put it, and how to start saving. Even office workers with no basic knowledge can accumulate money painlessly. The information in this article is updated as of May 2026.

What is an emergency fund? Why should every office worker have one?

The definition of emergency fund is simple: Cash that can sustain your basic life for 3-6 months even if you have no income at all. It is not an investment, it is not a savings insurance, it is not money placed in stocks, but cash that can be withdrawn at any time without losing money.

Why is it important? According to data from the Accounting and Accounting Office, the average window period between job changes for Taiwanese employees is about 2-4 months, and it may be longer than half a year for middle-aged and elderly people. In the event of being temporarily laid off, unable to work due to illness, or having an accident in the family, people without reserve funds can only make ends meet by swiping their credit cards, and the snowball of debt will get bigger and bigger.

The function of an emergency fund is to “allow you to maintain your options during low times.” You don’t have to take a bad job just to pay next month’s rent.

How much should you save? Calculation formula for 3-6 months living expenses

The most common rule is: 3 months for singles and 6 months for families. But how to calculate “one month’s living expenses”? It’s not your monthly salary, but “the money you have to spend in a month”, which usually includes:

  • Rent or mortgage
  • Water, electricity, gas, mobile Internet
  • Meal expenses (minimum standard for eating out or self-catering)
  • Transportation expenses
  • Insurance premiums (health insurance, labor insurance, life insurance, etc.)
  • Fixed loan monthly payment (car loan, student loan)

These add up to your “minimum cost of living.” Entertainment, dressing up, and dinner parties are not considered, and these can be suspended in an emergency.

Actual calculation of monthly salary of RMB 30,000 to RMB 60,000: How much money do you need?

Here are three typical examples:

Example A: Single person with a monthly salary of 30,000, renting a house

  • Rent 9,000 + Water, electricity and gas 1,500 + Food 8,000 + Transportation 2,000 + Insurance 2,000 = Monthly minimum 22,500
  • 3 months reserve fund = 67,500 yuan

Example B: Monthly salary of 45,000, renting a house with double income and no children (individual part)

  • Rent 15,000 (7,500 shared between two persons) + Water, electricity and gas 1,500 + Food 10,000 + Transportation 3,000 + Insurance 3,000 = Monthly minimum 25,000
  • 3 months reserve fund = 75,000 yuan

Example C: Monthly salary of 60,000, married with children

  • Mortgage 25,000 + Water, electricity and gas 2,500 + Food 18,000 + Childcare 12,000 + Transportation 4,000 + Insurance 5,000 = Monthly minimum 66,500
  • 6 months reserve fund = 399,000 yuan

Seems like a lot? In fact, as long as half of the monthly excess cash is automatically transferred to the reserve account, most people can save the target in 1-2 years. The key point is not “how many there are now”, but “has it started yet”.

How to start saving with zero foundation? Painless accumulation in three steps

First Month: Capture True Monthly Expenses

Dig out the credit card bills and EasyCard records for the past 3 months, add them up by category, and calculate the average monthly “necessary expenditures”. You can use Excel or accounting apps (MoneyGo, CWMoney, cumulative accounting) to classify.

Second month: Open a dedicated account

It is recommended to use a digital bank to open a special “Emergency Reserve” account, which is separate from the salary account. Richart, Yongfeng, Future, and Wangdao can all open accounts online in 5-10 minutes without having to visit a branch.

Starting from the third month: Set up automatic transfer

On payday, 10-20% of salary will be automatically transferred to this special account until the target amount of 3 or 6 months is reached.

If you want to quickly calculate how many months you need to save to reach your goal, you can use the Date Calculator to work out the target date and use it with the Countdown Timer to track the progress. It is easier to stick to it than relying solely on willpower.

Common misunderstanding: Don’t count this money into the emergency reserve fund

Many people mistakenly think that the following items can be used as reserve funds, but they find out at the critical moment that there is no money to use:

  • Credit Card Limit: This is a liability, not a deposit. If you use a credit card to pay for emergency expenses, the cash pressure will be greater next month. Credit card recurring interest rates of 7-15% (legal limit of 15%) will put you into a deeper debt cycle
  • Savings Insurance: You will lose money if you terminate it before it expires, and the liquidity is extremely poor.
  • Stocks, Funds: If you are forced to sell when the market is bad, you will realize losses instead.
  • Loan Promise from Relatives and Friends: When you really need money, others may not be able to lend it.
  • Estimated year-end bonus: It may be reduced or even canceled when the economy is bad, so it cannot be used as a stable source.

FAQ

Q1: The reserve fund has been saved for 6 months, do I still need to save it? A: No need. After you have saved enough, the excess money can be transferred to investments (stocks, ETFs, retirement withdrawals, etc.) to pursue higher returns. The role of reserve funds is “defense”, not “offense”. If you put too much, you will miss the opportunity cost. Re-examine it every year and adjust it to meet your living expenses.

Q2: Do people with mortgages need more reserve funds? A: Yes. A mortgage is a fixed and uninterruptible expense. It is recommended that those with a mortgage prepare at least 6 months of living expenses + a reserve fund for the mortgage. If the family only has a single salary, it is recommended to increase it to 9-12 months to avoid unemployment and the risk of supply cuts.

Q3: Can the reserve fund be used to pay card fees or invest? A: Strongly not recommended. Once the reserve fund is diverted to “non-emergency purposes”, the original insurance function is lost. If you really need to invest cash flow, you should separate an “investment account” from your salary and manage it completely separately from the reserve fund.

Q4: Can I invest at the same time while saving funds? A: Yes, but the order is important. It is recommended to first save a one-month reserve fund + pay off high-interest-rate liabilities, and then start small regular investments (for example, NT$3,000 per month in ETFs). Investing and saving funds at the same time can help maintain habits, but will not compress the quality of life due to excessive concentration.

Q5: Is NewNewBank 15% true? What are the traps? A: It is true, but there are restrictions: ① Only new accounts ② The limit is 100,000 ③ There is usually a “promotion period” of 3-6 months. Therefore, do not throw all your money into it as a long-term plan. It is suitable for the strategy of “enjoy high profits first, and then transfer to other long-term plans before expiration”.

Q6: Richart 3.5% is limited to new accounts, what should I do if I already have Richart? A: 1.8% (in the first half of 2026) for existing accounts within 300,000 yuan, which is still a relatively good living interest rate in the market. If you haven’t had a Richart account yet, it is recommended to open an account as soon as possible to enjoy the high interest rate of 3.5% in the first year** (annual interest gap: 300,000 × (3.5%-1.8%) = 5,100 yuan/year).

Q7: Will it be troublesome to decentralize multiple banks? A: Actually it’s not bad. Every bank app is easy to use, and setting up automatic transfers is done once. Only 1-2 operations are required per year (adjustment of new and old account discounts + review of interest rates at the beginning of the year). For an extra 1-2% interest rate, it’s worth spending half an hour setting it up.
