Save Money on a NT$35K Salary: 2026 Practical Guide for Young Workers in Taiwan

Personal Finance · March 22, 2026 · 8 min

Prices are rising and wages haven’t changed much. Can you still save money with a monthly salary of 35,000? Many people think it is “impossible”, but many young colleagues around me are using a few simple strategies to steadily save NT$8,000–12,000 per month in 2026. This article breaks down the four major expenditures of rent, meals, transportation, and savings, and gives you the latest median rent in 2026, TPASS 1,200 monthly pass, and Richart’s 3.5% high interest rate living and other practical figures.

1. It doesn’t matter if the salary is not much, the key is to spend it wisely

The annual CPI growth rate in Taiwan in 2026 is about 1.6%, and the price increase is moderate compared to the previous two years. However, office workers with a monthly salary of 30,000 to 40,000 yuan still need to be careful about every dollar they spend. Let me break down the monthly salary of 35,000 for you:

The key is that the “saving money version” is not about asceticism - it is about choosing the right plan for each expenditure, and then automatically transferring the money saved, so that you can spend the remaining money however you like without overspending. Let me break it down for you item by item below.

Real case: Miss A’s monthly salary NT$35,000 expense list

Miss A, 27 years old, shares a 3-bedroom apartment in Zhonghe, New Taipei (NT$8,000, including management fees, water and electricity), self-catering for 3 days + eating out for 4 days (meals NT$5,500), TPASS NT$1,200 monthly pass (NT$1,200), mobile phone + two subscriptions (NT$1,000), miscellaneous entertainment approx. NT$3,000, actual monthly savings: 35,000 − 8,000 − 5,500 − 1,200 − 1,000 − 3,000 = NT$16,300. Among them, NT$10,000 is automatically transferred to Richart to get 3.5%, NT$5,000 is fixed at 0050, and the remaining NT$1,300 is reserved for flexibility. The accumulated principal and interest of deposits in one year is about NT$130,000-140,000. After 5 years, with the compound interest of ETF, the assets can be accumulated to nearly NT$800,000.

2. Rent: 2026 Shared apartment vs. living in a home

Rent is the largest expense, and a little savings will be worth the savings on other items. The latest market trends in 2026:

  • Shared apartment in Shuangbei: 6,000–10,000 yuan (the last MRT sections such as West District, Xinzhuang, Shulin, and Yonghe are cheaper, while those in East District, Xinyi, and Banqiao business districts are on the higher side)
  • Independent Suite: 15,000–25,000 yuan (the elevator building includes management fees, and the rent per square meter in the essence of New Taipei District will exceed 1,000 yuan starting from 2025)
  • Monthly allowance for living at home: 3,000–5,000 yuan (the most economical, but it depends on the family situation)

Save money by renting a shared apartment, but you should pay attention to 3 things

  1. Second Landlord Risk: Confirm the identity of the landlord before signing the contract and ask to see the property tax bill or proof of property ownership.
  2. Lease Contract Legality: Only when it is legally registered as “residential use” can you claim the rent deduction
  3. The deposit must not exceed 2 months: According to the “Regulations on the Development and Management of the Rental Housing Market”, it is illegal to overcharge

Don’t forget to claim your rent deduction

Starting from 2026, the rent deduction amount will be increased to Special deduction amount of 180,000 yuan/year (effective retroactively from 2014). The monthly rent is NT$8,000 × 12 = 96,000, and the 5% tax rate applies to annual income below NT$500,000, which can save about NT$4,800 in tax refund. For details, see Complete Guide to 2026 Tax Filing.

3. Meal expenses: The real difference between self-cooking and eating out

Eating out for lunch + dinner every day costs NT$300, which is just over NT$9,000 a month. If you cook 3-4 days a week, buying food and gas costs about NT$3,000-4,000 a month, and you can save NT$4,000-5,000 by this alone**.

Actual measurement of self-cooking cost in 2026 (one month)

  • Vegetables (bought at traditional markets): 800–1,200 yuan (20% to 30% cheaper than supermarkets)
  • Meat/eggs (2kg chicken breast + 30 eggs per week): NT$1,200–1,500
  • Seasonings/Rice and noodles: 500–700 yuan
  • Water, electricity and gas bonus (multi-purpose for self-cooking): 300–500 yuan
  • Total: 2,800–3,900 yuan/month (26-30 meals in total for lunch and dinner)

How to save money on eating out

For those who cannot cook every day, you can use the “lunch box strategy”: choose a buffet for 90-110 yuan for lunch, and simply cook dinner at home, which costs about 200 yuan a day on average and 6,000 yuan a month.

Beverage fees are often underestimated

A cup of hand shake costs 50-70 yuan, and drinking 3 cups a week is 800-900 yuan a month. Switch to bringing your own coffee or tea bags and save more than 10,000 yuan a year.

4. Transportation: TPASS 1,200 monthly pass vs motorcycle

Many people think that “TPASS is only 1,200, is there any trap?” - No, this is a policy of central + local subsidies in 2023, stable operation from 2024, and TPASS 2.0 frequent guest discount will be launched on 2026-01-14.

Monthly ticket prices in each district

  • Beibei Jitao: 1,200 yuan (MRT + bus + Taiwan Railway section + some passenger transportation + YouBike free for the first 30 minutes)
  • Central (Zhongzhang Tou Miao): 699 or 999 yuan
  • South (Nangaoping): 999 yuan
  • Single County and City Plan: NT$399 in Tainan/Kaohsiung/Pingtung and other cities

TPASS 2.0 Frequent Customer Feedback (2026 onwards)

After registering the e-ticket to the Frequent Frequent Customer Discount Reward Area of the Highway Bureau, you can enjoy rewards for riding ≥ 11 times per month, and the reward ratio is graded according to the number of times. It’s like the ticket price has dropped again.

Regular Fixed Amount ETFs: The Long-Term Power of Compound Interest

Buy 0050 (Yanta Taiwan 50) or high dividend ETF (0056/00919) for a fixed monthly amount of NT$3,000–5,000. 0050’s annual interest-bearing return in the past five years is approximately 10.88%, which is far better than survival. 5,000 per month for 10 years (estimated at 8% compound interest) can accumulate to NT$920,000.

Autopay: The iron rule of saving first and spending later

Open the bank app, set up Salary Account → Savings Account for automatic transfer, and transfer NT$8,000–10,000 directly to Richart or NEXT the next day after payday. If you don’t see the money, you won’t spend it. This is more than 3 times more successful than “saving according to how much is left” at the end of the month.

6. FAQ

**Q1: ​​I am 30 years old and still earning a monthly salary of 35,000. Should I change my job? ** Saving money and changing jobs go hand in hand, not choosing one over the other. A monthly salary of 35,000 means that the market can still grow. You can also: ① Use the strategies in this article to save 10,000 per month to build a financial buffer. ② Learn new skills or take certifications to gradually increase your salary. The more buffer you have, the more likely you are to challenge for a higher-paying position when changing jobs.

**Q2: Can you really save so much by sharing a house? Will roommates be troublesome? ** You can save 5,000–10,000 by sharing a flat, but the running-in costs need to be taken into consideration. It is recommended to look for 2-3 room types, where tenants can work/rest independently (to prevent roommates from being unemployed at home), share utility bills/consumption of public supplies in writing, and make a clear agreement before moving in.

**Q3: Will the high interest rate remain so high? ** Won’t. Richart 3.5% is a limited-time project from 2026-03-02 to 2026-07-01; most banks’ high-interest deposits are “short-term projects” and may drop to 1.5-2% later. You need to monitor bank announcements regularly, or simply evaluate your account every 6 months.

**Q4: 0050 has risen a lot, is it too late to enter the market now? ** The core of regular quotas is to spread time risks. “Highest point vs. lowest point” has limited impact on long-term investors. The constituent stocks of 0050 are among the top 50 in Taiwan stock market in terms of market capitalization. They are growing in tandem with Taiwan’s economy and will not lose out to the market in a 5-10 year cycle. The key is not “when to enter the market” but “continue to enter the market”.

**Q5: 0050 vs 0056 vs 00919, which one should I choose? ** 0050 is a “market capitalization” type that pursues capital gains, with an annual interest rate of about 10.88% in the past five years and a yield rate of 1.39-2%; 0056 and 00919 are “high dividend” dividend distribution strategies, with a yield rate of 5-7% but weak capital gains. At the stage of accumulating principal for young people, it is recommended to mainly use 0050, with 1-2% of 0056/00919 to support life. Gradually switch to higher dividends as you approach retirement.

**Q6: Can credit cards be used? Will it be maxed out accidentally? ** The credit card itself is not guilty. The problem lies in “swiping without reconciliation.” Suggestions: ① Only apply for 1-2 cards with practical rewards (life bill card + overseas card) ② Reconcile the bill when it comes, and add reasons for all unexpected expenses ③ Set automatic full payment to avoid recurring interest (annual interest rate 7-15%) ④ Dynamically track the proportion of monthly credit card expenditures in salary, and be alert if it exceeds 30%.

7. 2026 Budget Review in an Inflationary Environment

In the past few years, you have been able to slowly save money by saving money and not investing, but the environment you will face in 2026 is different - you must actively reallocate your budget in line with inflation figures.

2026 Inflation Numbers: Mild But Sustained

Data from the General Accounting Office: Inflation rate was 1.75% in February 2026 and slowed to 1.2% in March. Although it is not like the peak of breaking 3% in 2022-2023, it still continues to eat up purchasing power. For an office worker with a monthly salary of 35,000, inflation alone will reduce your real purchasing power by about NT$5,000-8,000 per year, so “flat = regression”.

The relative position of minimum salary vs monthly salary of 35K in 2026

Starting from January 2026, the minimum salary will be adjusted to monthly salary NT$29,500 / hourly wage NT$196. A monthly salary of 35,000 is about 18% higher than the minimum wage and falls into the “slightly higher than the bottom” range. If your salary has not increased in 5 years, the real purchasing power will actually be lower than the 35K in 2020. This is not alarmist, it is purely numerical fact.

The key is “Automatic transfer and diversion” - let the system transfer the savings part first on the payday, and the rest is for living expenses. Save first and then spend is the thinking of the rich. Spending first and then saving only leaves you with debt.

”Minimum Implementation List” to Fight Inflation: Prioritize these 3 things

  1. Open source NT$1,000-3,000 per month: Take on 1-2 light side jobs (design, copywriting, administrative helper), the increase is much greater than the inflation of 1-2% (extended reading: [Side Job Recommendation Tools and Tax Guide] (/blog/en/freelance-side-hustle-tools/))
  2. Change to buy interest-bearing ETF: 0050 annualized interest rate 10.88% > inflation 1.2%, let the assets outperform prices on their own
  3. Avoid “fake discounts”: The “promotional prices” of mass merchandisers/online shopping often have built-in price increases. The actual unit price can only be seen by comparing the historical ratio.

Inflation is not the end of the world, but leaving it alone will slowly erode your efforts today. Take 5 minutes tonight to make a list of your subscriptions and evaluate whether you can cut off one or two — This is the fastest concrete action to show “I have combated inflation.”

in conclusion

If you want to save money with a monthly salary of 35,000, it’s not about doing hard work, it’s about choosing the right plan + automation**. You can save 5,000-10,000 by renting in a shared house or living at home, you can save 2,000-3,000 by using TPASS instead of motorcycle, you can save 4,000-5,000 by cooking for 3-4 days, and you can automatically transfer your salary to Richart and get 3.5% - the difference is NT$8,000-12,000 per month.

The money saved is processed with 3-6 months emergency reserve (high-interest living savings) + monthly fixed quota of 3,000-5,000 ETFs. After 10 years, the compound interest alone will accumulate to nearly one million. Money will not grow on its own, but it will earn interest on its own. First thing to do today: open your bank app and set up automatic transfers, things will be different starting next month. With 5 minutes of decision-making cost per month, options open up for the next ten years.
