How to Use Your 2026 Tax Refund Wisely: 5 Smart Allocation Strategies for Young Workers in Taiwan

Personal Finance · April 26, 2026 · 11 min

After filing taxes every May, what office workers look forward to most is the tax refund that will be credited at the end of July. According to the statistics released by the National Taxation Bureau over the years, the average tax refund amount per item is about 10,000 to 15,000 yuan (the first batch of 3.62 million people / 58.5 billion → an average of about 16,000 yuan in 2025; the average of the third batch in 2024 is about 11,000 yuan), and there may be more dual-income families. The problem is that once this “windfall” comes into the account, it will almost always be eaten up silently by eating, shopping, and watching TV series and subscriptions. This article provides 5 priorities for using tax refunds for small bourgeoisie with a monthly salary of 30,000 to 60,000 yuan. It also attaches examples of specific allocation ratios and decision-making examples. If you follow these steps, the tax refunds can truly help your finances. The information in this article is updated as of May 2026.

Why shouldn’t tax refund money be spent directly?

Many people will say, “The tax refund is not in the budget to begin with, so it would be a pity to spend it.” This concept has two blind spots:

  • The tax refund is not money given to you by the government: It is the salary tax that was withheld every month from you, which is equivalent to “forced saving for one year” and you will get it back in one go.
  • Single large amounts are the easiest to develop bad habits: The “mental accounting effect” in behavioral economics will make people take sudden money less seriously, and the actual purchase decision will be 2-3 times more relaxed than usual.

Treat the tax refund as the “second year-end bonus of the year” and establish a distribution formula to accumulate considerable assets every year in the future. The point is to deduct first and then spend, not spend first and then see how much is left. Calculated from the annual tax refund of 15,000, if you invest seriously for 10 consecutive years, the tax refund alone can accumulate at least 150,000 principal plus compound interest income.

No. 1: Build a 3-6 month emergency reserve

The emergency reserve fund is the basis of all financial management and can support life in the event of unemployment, illness, or family emergency. The recommended amount is 3 to 6 times your monthly living expenses:

  • Monthly salary of 30,000 yuan: emergency fund of 90,000 to 180,000 yuan
  • Monthly salary of 40,000: emergency fund of 120,000 to 240,000
  • Monthly salary of 50,000: emergency fund of 150,000 to 300,000
  • Monthly salary 60,000: emergency fund 180,000 to 360,000

If you don’t have an emergency fund yet, 100% of your tax refund will be invested in this account. It is recommended to place high-interest savings in digital banks (such as Taishin Richart 3.5% annual interest for new accounts within 300,000, Wing Fung DAWHO, Future Bank, etc.), which can not only receive interest but can be used at any time. A complete emergency fund usually takes 1-2 years to accumulate. Don’t rush to buy stocks or funds yet. First, 1 month of living expenses will be used as the “minimum safety allowance”, and then it will be paid up to 3-6 months in stages.

Second pick: Pay off high-interest card debt and credit

If you have a recurring credit card (annual interest rate of 7-15%, legal upper limit of 15%, most banks have been between 13-15% in recent years), cash cards, or credit (annual interest rate of 5-12%, depending on personal credit history), the best use of tax refund money is to pay off debt. The reason is simple:

  • High-interest savings 3.5% interest rate vs. credit card debt interest rate 13-15%, the gap is more than 4 times
  • Early repayment is the most certain “rate of return” and there is no market risk
  • After the debt is reduced, the monthly cash flow immediately becomes wider and the credit score recovers.

There are two debt repayment strategies. Which one you choose depends on the easiest way for you to stick to it:

  • Avalanche Method: Prioritize repayment with the highest interest rate (for example, the card with 14% repayment first). The most cost-effective financial solution, the total interest can be saved by 20-30% in 5 years
  • Snowball Method: Pay off the debt with the smallest balance first (for example, pay off the small debt of 8,000 yuan at once). High sense of psychological achievement, suitable for people who easily give up halfway

For example: You have two debts, credit card A is 60,000 yuan / interest rate 14%, and credit card B is 10,000 yuan / interest rate 7%. The avalanche method prioritizes paying off A (saving the most interest), and the snowball method prioritizes paying off B (quickly seeing a debt return to zero). It is recommended that people with a monthly salary of 30,000 to 40,000 yuan and small debts use the snowball method. When they see it return to zero, they will be more motivated to stick to it.

Third pick: Enrich savings insurance and time deposits

If your emergency fund is ready and you have no credit card debt, the next step is to allocate your tax refund to medium-term savings tools:

  • Digital Bank High Interest Savings: 3.5% for new Richart accounts within 300,000 yuan, 1.6% for new Wing Fung DAWHO accounts within 500,000 yuan, 2.5% for new bank accounts within 300,000 yuan in the future, some accounts can reach 2% to 3.5%
  • 1-year fixed deposit: The annual interest rate is about 1.6% (Taiwan dollars), suitable for funds that are not used in the short term; the U.S. dollar fixed deposit is about 4-4.5%, but there is exchange rate risk
  • Savings insurance (more than 6 years): Annualized rate of return 1.5% to 2.5%, locked interest rate, but poor liquidity, cancellation in the first 6 years will result in a loss of 5-15%; if the money may be used in the next 6 years, fixed deposits are more suitable

For example: If you save 1 million for a 6-year savings insurance at the age of 30, if you need money urgently to terminate the contract in the third year, you may only get back 880,000 to 920,000, which is equivalent to a loss of 80,000 to 120,000 in principal. It is recommended to leave at least 50,000 to 100,000 in a digital bank high-interest account as a “semi-emergency fund”, somewhere between emergency funds and investments.

No. 4: Try index ETF or monthly dividend distribution

After accumulating emergency funds, tax refund money can be invested. The most friendly tool for petty bourgeoisie is Taiwan Stock Index ETF:

  • 0050 (Yanta Taiwan 50): Tracks the top 50 companies in Taiwan by market capitalization, The annualized return in the past five years is approximately 10.88% (2020-2024 range)
  • 006208 (Fubon Channel 50): Similar to 0050, the total expense ratio is lower (about 0.24% vs 0.43% of 0050)
  • 00878, 00919, 00940 and other high dividend ETFs: suitable for conservatives who pursue quarterly dividend distribution, with a yield rate of about 5-7%

A tax refund of 10,000 yuan can buy 1-2 shares of 00878 (about 21 yuan per share), or you can accumulate 0050 in odd shares (about 220 yuan per share → a tax refund of 10,000 yuan can buy about 45 shares). Before investing, ask yourself: **Will this money be used in the next 3 years? **If so, don’t buy stocks; short-term fluctuations in the stock market may make you panic and sell at lows. The best practice is to buy at regular fixed amounts in 2-4 times to average costs and reduce time-point risks.

No. 5: Invest in yourself (learning and health)

If the above four items have been taken care of, the last tax refund can be invested in yourself:

  • Online courses: AI tools (ChatGPT / Claude / Cursor), presentations, English, financial report analysis, you can buy a good course for 1,000 to 5,000 yuan
  • Health Checkup: Over the age of 30, a complete health checkup every 2 years at your own expense costs about 10,000 yuan. Early detection and early treatment of diseases are better than treatment.
  • Sports Equipment or Membership: Annual gym fee, running shoes, yoga mat, to establish exercise habits
  • Professional Certificate: registration examination fees, teaching material fees, and increased salary negotiation chips

Investing in yourself often has the highest rate of return, but only if you really know how to use it. It is a waste to buy an online course and never open it, or to apply for a gym card and only go to it three times. It is recommended to try it out for a week first, and then buy the paid version if you are sure that you will continue.

Example of tax refund allocation ratio (5,000 / 10,000 / 30,000 yuan)

The following are suggestions for the allocation of different tax refund amounts, assuming you already have some emergency funds and no credit card debt:

  • Tax refund of 5,000 yuan: Invest all 5,000 yuan in emergency funds or digital bank high-interest savings (richart new account 3.5% annual interest, receive about 175 yuan interest in one year)
  • Tax refund 10,000 yuan: 6,000 yuan emergency fund + 3,000 yuan ETF odd shares (can buy about 13 shares of 0050 or about 142 shares of 00878) + 1,000 yuan for self-investment (course subscription)
  • Tax refund of RMB 30,000: RMB 10,000 emergency fund + RMB 10,000 ETF + RMB 5,000 savings insurance + RMB 5,000 health check-up or course

Before planning, you may wish to use the Exchange Rate Conversion Tool to see how many shares you can buy if the tax refund money is converted into US dollars to invest in overseas ETFs such as VOO or VT; or to evaluate the pros and cons of retaining Taiwan dollars. Once you create your own distribution formula, it will be executed automatically every July, eliminating the need for any extra thought.

FAQ

Q1: Will the tax refund be credited to my account by the end of July?

uncertain. Only those who completed online declarations from May 1 to May 10 without errors will receive tax refunds in the first batch (end of July). Those who file after May 11th will have to wait until the end of October, and those who submit supplementary documents or manual review will not be credited until January 20th of the following year at the latest. According to announcements by the National Taxation Bureau over the years, the amount credited to the first batch accounts for about 70-80% of the total tax refund households, while the third batch only accounts for about 50,000-70,000 households.

Q2: I have credit card debt and insufficient emergency funds. Should I pay off the debt first or save money first?

It is recommended to save the “minimum safety fund” (1 month’s living expenses) first to avoid taking on new debts during the debt repayment process (for example: no emergency fund → sudden toothache and need for dental implant → forced to swipe credit card → new debt). After that, priority will be given to repaying high-interest debts with interest rates above 13%, and the remainder will be used to supplement emergency funds. Do both in parallel to avoid falling into a debt cycle. If your monthly salary is less than 30,000 yuan and your credit card debt exceeds 300,000 yuan, you may consider applying for debt negotiation (the interest rate is reduced to 5-9%) and adding tax refunds to speed up the repayment.

Q3: Can I use my tax refund money to buy ETFs?

Not recommended. Unless you have an emergency fund set aside and won’t need it in the next 5 years. If an investment encounters a sharp drop (2008 / 2020 / 2022 have all fallen by 20-40%), the psychological pressure will be great, and it is easy to accept losses at the low point and rush out. It is recommended to buy ** in regular fixed amounts in 2-4 times to spread the time-point risks and lower the average cost.

Q4: Is savings insurance really cost-effective? Is it better than a fixed deposit?

The annualized rate of return of savings insurance is usually slightly higher than that of fixed deposits (about 1.5-2.5% vs 1.5-1.6% for fixed deposits), but the liquidity is much poorer, and the loss of capital will be 5-15% if canceled in the first 6 years**. If you are likely to use this money within 6 years, a fixed deposit or a digital bank high-interest deposit is more suitable. Savings insurance is suitable for people who “know that they will not use it for more than 10 years + want to force savings”, such as setting aside education funds for their children and insuring themselves for retirement at 65.

Q5: Can tax refund funds be used to directly purchase virtual currency or cryptocurrency?

Not recommended as primary configuration. Cryptocurrencies are extremely volatile (Bitcoin fell from 60,000 US dollars to 16,000 US dollars in 2022, a drop of 73%), and are not suitable for use as stable assets. If you are really interested, it is recommended that you only use 5-10% of the tax refund as “entertainment money” to enter the market (for example, if you have a tax refund of 10,000 yuan, only use 1,000 yuan), and choose mainstream currencies (BTC/ETH) instead of small coins; at the same time, remember that “you can only enter the market if you can lose all the money”, and never use emergency funds or borrow money to buy coins.

A tax refund is not just a lump sum of money, but a good time to review your financial health. Read [2026 Comprehensive Tax Declaration Complete Guide] (/blog/en/tax-filing-guide-2026/) before your tax refund is credited to your account to confirm how much you should refund; after it is credited, follow the steps in step 5 of this article. Over a period of 5-10 years, the tax refund can be converted into a considerable asset base, rather than another consumption opportunity.
